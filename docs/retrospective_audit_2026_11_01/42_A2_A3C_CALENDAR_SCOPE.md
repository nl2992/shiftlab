# A2/A3c calendar timing claims: archive arithmetic (partial W01/W02/W08)

`audit_a2_a3c_archive.py` recomputed the rational joint-versus-best-pure-calendar gaps in all 162 stored A2 screen cells and matched `summary.json`. The registered 54 HMM families each have risk levels `1/20`, `1/10`, and `1`; all 108 tight-risk cells have `J = C*` exactly as stored. Thirty-one of the 54 unconstrained cells have a positive gap, so the tight-risk zeros are not merely a universal equality of these planners. A2's historical `CONFINED TO LOOSE ALPHA` screen decision follows the registered rule: no family survived the required risk-specific excess of 0.005. The calculation does not rerun the LP solver or prove the stored optima are globally correct.

The A3c calendar-value and summary files match their recorded SHA-256 hashes. All six reported `alpha = 0.10`, `B = 4` mixture-gap rational lower/upper pairs are positive and ordered, with ranges 0.712–1.173% for lower bounds and 0.796–1.213% for upper bounds. Their unconstrained gaps range 2.710–5.557%, larger in each matched family. Thus these particular stored calculations support a small gap at the programme risk level, but not a *risk-specific amplification* over the unconstrained case. This is only an archive rational/float and bound-order check; it does not independently solve the 8,316 calendar-value rows, validate the continuous-envelope lower-bound proof, or establish a general HMM theorem.

The related F4 tight-risk structural claim cites Proposition 6 and Theorem 7 and additional A3 cells; those proofs and per-instance certificates were not independently reviewed here. The F5 intermediate-risk 5.066% and 4.682% lower values were previously reproduced from A1d's stored grid in `21_A1D_ARITHMETIC_AUDIT.md`, with independent LP and continuous-bound proof still open. F7's “do not pay for joint planning” is a research-priority interpretation of a limited model family, not a universal dominance or deployment rule. None of these historical decisions is retroactively changed.
