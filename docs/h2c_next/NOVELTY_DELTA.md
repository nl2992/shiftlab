# Novelty delta (no prior-art search possible: paper hosts blocked; nothing below is a novelty claim)
**Exact modification.** H2c tests the decision-cost null with a prediction-powered increment, and picks the bet fraction from the *discounted Kelly fraction of the Brier-risk increment built on the same labels*, rescaled to the cost-increment's admissible interval. Both increments are bounded, IPW-corrected and predictable; the cost e-process is valid under its own null (fixed or independent delay).
**Known ingredients:** prediction-powered/IPW increments, bounded-mean betting (Waudby-Smith–Ramdas), Kelly/ONS bet selection, exponential discounting, e-process Ville bound. **Closest prior art not read:** PPRM, WATCH, ASI/Robust ASI, "growth-oriented querying" (snippets only). Using one test statistic to choose the bet for a different one is a form of side information / mixture betting that may already be covered; I cannot rule it out.
**Empirical delta (development and one frozen G5 confirmation):** +0.09 power over the strongest cost-null comparators implemented (cost-only discounted Kelly, cost-only ONS) at a cost-null false-alarm profile. **Limitations:** the comparators are local adaptations, not published methods; one underlying generator family; no real-data test; no theorem of optimality or growth-rate improvement (CONJECTURED).
