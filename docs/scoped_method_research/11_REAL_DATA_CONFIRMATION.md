## Retrospective source-cluster evidence

Corrected replays on previously exposed constructed streams show Gated+front versus Gated mean
source-cluster `J(front) − J(Gated) = −24.55`, 95% source-level t interval [−42.81, −6.29], across
14 dataset-source clusters. The same front schedule is indistinguishable from tuned pace
(`−0.30`, interval [−1.05, +0.45]), C2 (`+0.60`, [−1.15, +2.35]) and C6 (`+0.03`, [−3.07, +3.14]).
Five-source and nine-source cohorts separately have wide or exposed intervals. These results favor a
simple acquisition schedule on average against unmodified Gated in this retrospective replay; they
do not validate a new method or demonstrate transfer to independently sourced deployments.

Raw paired traces, source effects, exposure labels, delays and run hashes are in

its JSON artifacts. Inference is clustered at source, not episode. The 14 clusters include related
domains and are exposed, and the label clocks are imposed. They do not meet the frozen target
registry/chronology gate.

## 2026-11-06 append-only confirmation update

One Wild-Time HuffPost source was opened once after a validation-only eligibility gate and a frozen
source/code/model manifest. This does **not** change the primary method-contribution status: no new
repair was validated, the label clock was simulated at publication date + 24 hours, and the study
contains one source cluster. On the 3,877-row test stream, PFA-SR spent 200 labels, never promoted,
and cost 3,163; same-host RMM cost 2,039 and Xu acquisition-only cost 1,454. The paired cost
intervals are unfavorable to PFA-SR versus both comparators. UCI and MN-DS were validation-ineligible
and were not opened. Accordingly, this is a negative single-source case study, not C4 confirmation.
The original “no confirmation” assessment remains correct for a new method claim and is retained as
historical provenance. The full result and limitations are in
`../scoped_method_research_2026-11-06/REAL_DATA_AUDIT.md`.
