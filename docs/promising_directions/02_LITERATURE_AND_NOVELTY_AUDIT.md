## Mechanism-level equivalence verdicts (repo-supported, not new proofs)

- **H2c:** repo matrix Gate A: a composition of A2 / A3 increments, predictable discounted Kelly, guide-based bet fraction and A1 alpha-split; reduces exactly (max |dlogW| about 9e-16) to cost-only Kelly when the guide is the cost statistic; validity is the generic predictable-betting argument; no growth theorem. Novelty claim withdrawn in the repo.
- **HG:** A10 stratified composition plus A2 / A3. Standard.
- **DQ (disagreement / structural-zero querying):** known elements are A3 propensity design, A5 growth-oriented querying, A7 active cap, A8 disagreement acquisition, B5 / B6 pacing. DISCERN (Balachandran, arXiv 2609.17560; repo-cited, not opened by me) already pays only for disagreement. The unchanged published algorithms A4, A5, A12 are excluded by information contract rather than beaten.
- **JP:** on a finite hidden-state model it is a belief-MDP or constrained POMDP (C9, C10, C7, C8, B7); the repo's two-law sequence-form LP is a classical solver. Generic planner is prior art (repo G1 gate).
- **PB:** A9 / C11 and Off-Policy Confidence Sequences and Conformal Policy Control (repo `closest_prior_work.md`, not opened by me) cover generic gated promotion. Future net benefit net of switching cost under non-persistent improvement is the repo's stated residual.

## Is the "joint vs separate acquisition and switching gap" explained by existing theory?

**Qualitatively yes, quantitatively no.** Three bodies of theory predict that jointly optimized acquisition and action can strictly beat independently optimized components: (1) team decision theory, where person-by-person optimality does not imply team optimality outside Radner's static convex class, and Witsenhausen's counterexample shows failure under non-classical information structures (D1, D2; primary texts not read by me); (2) the estimation / control separation literature, where actions that influence future information break separation (D3, bibliographic level); (3) value of information, where joint value need not equal the sum of parts (D5). Stochastic probing (D4) shows adaptivity gaps can be bounded by constants for submodular objectives, a template for a magnitude statement. None of these gives the size, structure or absence of the gap in this model (hard pathwise cap, fixed delay d >= 1, any-promotion conditional-null risk, recurrent reversible switching). So the *existence* of a positive joint-vs-separate gap is not a novelty claim; only a proven separation theorem or bound in this model could be. The repo's one solved small cell showed cohort equivalence (no adaptive acquisition advantage), per `switching_paid_observation_audit_v1.md`.

## Residual novelty space

At most three precise questions not covered by any source verified here:

1. **Separation theorem in the exact model.** With a hard request-time cap B, fixed delay d >= 1 and multiple pending queries, a global any-promotion conditional-null risk <= alpha (adaptive nature), and recurrent reversible switching with switching cost, is there a family where the best composition of (sequential-design acquisition) + (separately valid anytime test) + (economic switching rule) has a provably strictly positive utility gap to the exact joint planner, and what is the tight order of that gap?
2. **Persistence-aware certificate.** Is there a time-uniform guarantee on the future net benefit of the selected replacement (net of switching and query cost) over a post-switch horizon, when the improvement need not persist, under delayed adaptively acquired paired feedback? Every certificate located bounds a historical or running-average quantity.
3. **Scalable guarantee for the two-law constrained planner.** Does any structure beyond generic exponential history trees give a polynomial-time controller with a provable value gap to the exact two-law sequence-form optimum for this risk-constrained delayed problem?

Caveat: A6, A8, A9, A13, C1, C2, C8 and the Qiu reference are unread or unlocated; read them in full before asserting these questions are open. Action-dependent (chosen-arm-only) feedback in paired-harm monitoring is a further repo-noted residual, not counted above.
