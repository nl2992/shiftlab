## Open decisions

1. Which of WATCH, PPRM, Prediction-Powered E-Values, and paired-score confidence sequences can be reproduced on one shared paired-cost feedback task without changing their original estimand?
2. Which real dataset can support a defensible development task and a genuinely held-out confirmation contract? Current CRSP, Gas Sensor, WILDS, Folktables, EIA and OPSD evidence does not establish this; Criteo Attribution is unsuitable for informative within-stream monitoring under the current assumed maturity convention.
3. Can one intervention benchmark compare full-feedback fixed policies with action-dependent feedback methods fairly, or should these be separated into two study panels?
4. Once reproduction and real-data development results exist, is the contribution a unified method, a narrower benchmark, one targeted improvement, or a pivot/no-go?

No optimization branch should begin until these decisions are answered with the registered evidence required by the objective.
