# B1 P4 persistence-aware promotion: scoped Q2 negative (partial W01/W02/W03)

The B1 protocol preregistered Q2 as a comparison of P4 against P3 under matched 2,000-event streams, hard label budgets 200/400, switch charges 5/20, fixed release delay 5, and four synthetic worlds. P4 adds a known-hazard persistence hurdle (`hazard_mean=150`) to P3's evidence; its decision-theoretic novelty is explicitly limited by that plug-in assumption. The Q2 `SUPPORTED` gate required at least 10% lower P4 cost in reversing world W3 with a paired interval excluding zero, plus no more than 2% higher cost in useful-persistent world W1.

`audit_b1_p4.py` verified the archived `summary.json` and `per_seed.npz` hashes and independently recomputed all four Q2 settings from 500 paired seeds with the registered 2,000-resample bootstrap index. Every archived mean, interval, relative cost, and gate flag matches exactly. The W3 cost improvements are only 0.24%, 0.39%, 0.62%, and 0.82%; the paired P4-minus-P3 cost intervals are [-1.406, 0.050], [-2.012, -0.134], [-2.888, -0.480], and [-3.660, -0.750]. Three of the four intervals exclude zero, but none approaches the registered 10% materiality bar. In W1, P4 costs 10.45%, 22.33%, 9.50%, and 23.93% more than P3, violating the 2% guardrail in all four settings. The historical `NOT SUPPORTED` and stop decision are reproduced; the small W3 benefits should remain visible as descriptive tradeoffs, not be relabeled as a protocol pass.

This checks archived seed arithmetic, not the full simulator, conditional-null proof, generalization to other hazards, or external baselines. P4 was dropped from B2, so B2 does not independently confirm or reverse this P4-specific result.
