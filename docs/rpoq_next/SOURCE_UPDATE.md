## Consequences
1. **Candidate A is not novel.** The core idea (querying chosen to maximize e-value or supermartingale growth, mixed with uniform exploration) appears in Hsu and Shekhar, in e-PS (per the repo audit), and in this repo's own myopic rule. The per-context-bet variant remains an unchecked detail only.
2. **Our central empirical finding is independently corroborated:** two 2026 papers report that uniform querying is competitive with or better than adaptive/uncertainty-based querying in sequential active PPI settings. A benchmark/negative-result framing ("when and why uniform is hard to beat") is better supported by the literature than any new allocation claim.
3. **Candidate B** (robust hybrid) is partly occupied (Robust ASI for estimation; Hsu and Shekhar's mixtures). A sequential-e-value analogue with a guarantee is not seen in the snippets but cannot be ruled out without full text.
