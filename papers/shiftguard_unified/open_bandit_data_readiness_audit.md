# Open Bandit Dataset readiness audit

**Audit date:** 2026-10-26  
**Status:** full official archive acquired; complete Random/all log converted to Parquet; complete BTS/all log streamed into aggregate checks; not yet an eligible confirmation run.

## Availability and provenance

ZOZO Research describes OBD as real logged bandit feedback from an A/B experiment and provides a full archive of approximately 26 million item-placement records. The full archive README identifies the data as CC BY 4.0. The small sample's README does not repeat a license statement; retain source attribution and check the archive's attribution terms before redistribution. Raw archive and processed observations are ignored by Git. The archive SHA-256, source README hash and processing results are recorded in `data/reports/open_bandit_full_acquisition_manifest.json`; the 10,000-row sample hashes remain in `data/reports/open_bandit_small_source_manifest.json`.

The audit acquired the `all` campaign's small `random` and `bts` logs from the official `st-tech/zr-obp` repository at commit `8cbd5fa4558b7ad2ba4781546d6604e4cc3e07c4`. Each has 10,000 rows and 90 columns, no missing cells, 80 item IDs and positions 1–3. Timestamps parse with explicit UTC suffixes and cover 24–30 November 2019. There are 9,991 unique timestamps in the random sample and 9,999 in the BTS sample; only 18 and 2 rows respectively share a timestamp, and the samples share no exact timestamp. This does not identify a user or a full recommendation slate.

The random logger has a constant recorded marginal propensity of 0.0125 (1/80) in all three positions, positive support for all 80 items, and a 0.38% row-level click rate. The BTS logger has positive propensities in this sample, but they range from 0.000045 to 0.95424 (1st percentile 0.000955), with a 0.42% click rate. Its importance weights can therefore be extremely large. The per-position action and outcome counts, raw hashes, and exact timestamp coverage are in the generated manifest. The report contains aggregate metadata only. The source-only manifest lives under `data/reports/`; it is separate from `data/manifests/`, whose entries are processed datasets consumed by the shared validation/report pipeline.

The full official archive (version `08/18/2020 Open Bandit Dataset 1.0`) is locally acquired at 412,931,917 compressed bytes (SHA-256 `e8ec18196582a5937381a1776382ca940689b90a18d2dcd1fb635be6df614d78`; 11,746,769,342 bytes uncompressed). Random/all was streamed into 1,374,327 Parquet rows across 28 row groups (90 columns; 13,729,999 bytes), with 0.3469% click rate over seven UTC days and constant 1/80 logged propensity. BTS/all was streamed to aggregate checks across 12,357,200 rows, with 0.4953% click rate over seven UTC days. The BTS count differs by 189,116 from the paper's 12,168,084 all-campaign count. A version update is plausible because the official release page describes a 26-million-row dataset, but it gives no component count or checksum resolving the difference. Treat the version reconciliation as open. The processing script is `scripts/prepare_open_bandit_full.py`; the manifest records member counts, hashes and daily/position aggregates. The small sample remains plumbing-only, not a powered experiment.

## Research fit

OBD is the strongest currently identified candidate for a real-data **Q2 policy-value and candidate-certification** comparison. It records chosen item, position, click reward and logged action probability. The source paper treats the three positions as a slate and explicitly assumes the click reward depends on item and position; it warns that interactions between simultaneously shown items may violate this assumption. The paper benchmarks DM, IPS, SNIPS, DR, SNDR, Switch-DR, DRos and MRDR, and reports degraded OPE accuracy in its out-of-sample setting. ShiftLab's full-archive IPS/SNIPS run estimates Random from BTS and checks against the concurrently recorded Random logger. Overall IPS was within 1.03% of the Random reference, but its day-by-position mean absolute error was 0.000553 and position-2/3 aggregate ESS fractions were about 0.115%. A cross-fitted item-position DM/DR supplement finds DM within 0.14% overall and DR within 3.90%; DR does not improve on IPS in this run, and date/position errors remain uneven. These are point estimates, not a stable estimator ranking or candidate-intervention result. See `open_bandit_ope_development_results.md`.

The dataset does **not** close the Q1 or prospective-streaming requirement. Its timestamps are impression times; click-release and feature-delivery times are not documented in the inspected source. The sampled records do not expose a user/session or complete-slate identifier, so uncertainty must respect the logging unit and any within-slate dependence must be handled according to the official task representation. The 7-day collection period supplies only seven coarse time blocks. The 80 `user-item_affinity_*` features encode past-click information but their as-of construction is not established here; exclude them from learners until verified. The click indicator is a real platform outcome, but it is not revenue, conversion, user welfare, or net business utility.

## Eligibility decision

**GO for fixed-horizon, offline OPE development using the acquired archive, after freezing and auditing its contract. NO-GO for online/anytime-valid monitoring or a real-world net-utility claim.** Start with the Random logger because its 1/80 marginal propensity is bounded and uniform; predeclare candidate policies, action-space support and the exact slate/position estimand. Include direct and doubly robust OPE baselines, a conservative policy-improvement comparator where its assumptions apply, the logger value as an external reference only after resolving the BTS count discrepancy, and a never-change policy. Reserve whole time blocks before inspecting policy outcomes; disclose that this leaves few temporal clusters. Report click value separately from any imposed switching/resource costs.

Do not place raw OBD files in Git. Feature-availability verification, slate-contract verification, a conservative policy comparison, an independent temporal holdout, and reconciliation of the BTS row count remain open gates. Current estimates have no confidence interval and no prospective label-release clock. The full archive runner is `scripts/prepare_open_bandit_full.py`; the IPS/SNIPS runner is `scripts/evaluate_open_bandit_random_ope.py`; the cross-fitted DM/DR runner is `scripts/evaluate_open_bandit_dr_ope.py`; and the small-sample audit runner is `scripts/audit_open_bandit_sample.py`. The runners report aggregate metadata and checksums.
