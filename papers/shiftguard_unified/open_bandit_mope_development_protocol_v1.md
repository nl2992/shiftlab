# OBD fixed-candidate MOPE development replay v1

**Status:** frozen before the new split-specific analysis; exploratory real-data development, not confirmation.  
**Date:** 2026-10-26  
**Question:** On a held-forward portion of the Open Bandit Random/all log, does the source-pinned MOPE gated-deployment confidence sequence ever certify that a fixed, training-selected item policy improves click reward over the uniform Random logger, and how does that compare with a fixed-horizon bounded-score gate?

## Why this task is eligible for a limited replay

The OBD source log includes a selected item, position, binary click reward, UTC impression timestamp, and the behavior propensity. The official source documents the Random logger's uniform item assignment. In this release the recorded Random propensity is `1/80`, so a deterministic one-item target at one fixed display position has importance ratio `w ∈ {0, 80}`. This gives the MOPE gated-deployment process a known weight bound without estimating it from the evaluation outcomes. The task is a per-item-position click-value comparison, not slate utility or net business value.

The primary MOPE paper uses IID contextual-bandit observations. OBD's seven-day sequence has no user/session or complete-slate key, and its impression times do not verify feature or click-release availability. Potential repeated-user, within-slate, time, or policy-feedback dependence cannot be ruled out. Therefore this replay tests method execution and descriptive behavior on real logged data under the stated IID/no-interference interpretation; it **does not validate or claim the confidence-sequence guarantee on OBD**. The outcome split was not previously used for this exact target policy, but the full week has already appeared in ShiftLab's earlier OPE aggregates and daily diagnostics, so the evaluation dates are exposed development data, not a final holdout.

## Frozen information and policy contract

- **Source:** official Open Bandit Dataset 1.0, Random/all, processed file and checksums recorded in `data/reports/open_bandit_full_acquisition_manifest.json`.
- **Decision unit:** one logged item-placement record at position 1. Other displayed positions remain generated by the Random logger. The source's item-position outcome factorization/no-interference assumption is required; a complete slate is not observed in the released row representation.
- **Training dates:** UTC dates 2019-11-24 through 2019-11-26, inclusive.
- **Evaluation dates:** UTC dates 2019-11-27 through 2019-11-30, inclusive, in timestamp order. Do not tune the policy on these outcomes.
- **Incumbent/logger `h`:** uniform Random item at position 1, with logged action probability `1/80`.
- **Candidate `π`:** choose the single item with the largest empirical position-1 click rate on training dates. Break exact ties by smallest item ID. The policy ignores user and affinity features; no feature whose as-of availability is unresolved is consumed.
- **Reward:** binary item click in `[0,1]`. It is an observed platform outcome, not revenue, welfare, or a business KPI.
- **Weight and score:** `w=80` if the logged item is the frozen candidate, otherwise `w=0`; `Z=wR-R` is in `[-1,79]` with known range width `80`.
- **Feedback convention:** the offline file's click label is supplied in event order to the replay. Actual historical click-release time is unknown; this is not a prospective or delayed-feedback test.
- **Costs:** report event/label count and stop time separately. No click-to-dollar conversion or total net-utility claim.
- **Method:** unmodified `wealth_lb_gd` from the pinned author MOPE repository at commit `a4b8584b622f3a8296932568c520c1bb3e1957f0`; `wmin=0`, `wmax=80`, one-sided level `alpha=0.05` for the lower confidence sequence of `V(π)-V(h)`.
- **Comparator:** at the fixed final evaluation horizon, one-sided Hoeffding lower bound using the same `Z` values and range width `80`; no optional stopping is claimed for this fixed-N comparator.

## Endpoints and analysis

Primary endpoints are (1) whether the MOPE lower confidence sequence crosses zero before any empty confidence set, (2) the first crossing event or censored evaluation horizon, and (3) the final fixed-N Hoeffding promotion decision. An empty MOPE set is reported as a method failure and blocks later promotion. Report the final IPW difference estimate, candidate item, training/evaluation counts, realized incumbent click rate, maximum observed ratio, per-day descriptive estimates, and confidence-sequence endpoints at each UTC day boundary.

The OBD stream is one historical realization; do not manufacture replication-level confidence intervals by resampling its rows. The MOPE band is displayed with its formal IID interpretation but is explicitly **not** treated as a real-data-valid interval here. The fixed-N Hoeffding band has the same IID limitation. No p-value, generalization claim, or superiority claim will be inferred from one stream.

## Resource and stopping rule

Use the existing processed Parquet file and pinned reference code; read only timestamp, item ID, position, propensity, and click. Keep only position 1 and the frozen date window in memory. The MOPE reference calculation runs on CPU; no GPU workload is required for this scalar sequential optimizer. Stop after one complete, checksum-verified replay, unless input integrity or numerical validation fails. Do not change the target or dates in response to evaluation outcomes.

## Validation and provenance requirements

The runner must refuse output overwrite; verify source and manifest hashes, expected schema/counts/date range, all selected propensities, target support, `w∈{0,80}`, reward range, timestamp order, MOPE source commit, output lengths, finite values, monotonic lower/upper paths, and any empty confidence set. An independent verifier must reconstruct the candidate from training data, regenerate the evaluation score stream, recompute summary values and artifacts, and confirm the evaluation date/count boundaries. Save aggregate-only metrics and a manifest with protocol, source, method, and output hashes; do not persist learner-level feature or outcome records.

## Decision rule

If the source contract or weights fail validation, mark the run ineligible and stop. Otherwise execute once and report the result as an exploratory retrospective policy-gate replay. Whether or not MOPE promotes, retain all negative findings and keep OBD out of final confirmation until the unit/dependence, feature timing, label timing, source-version discrepancy, and prior-exposure issues are resolved.
