\section{Full Panel and Cohort Description}\label{app:panel}

\paragraph{Policies (20).} Arms: \texttt{NAD2S-g10-f6}, \texttt{PB80-g10}, \texttt{NAD-m80-g10}.
Dependence-robust comparators: A5b, D5 (V2), QSG-env8. Published-certificate ports:
\texttt{gated\_deployment\_native}, \texttt{gated\_adaptive\_range\_native},
\texttt{gated\_adaptive\_range\_native+front}, \texttt{gated\_adaptive\_range} (quad endpoint),
\texttt{discern\_style}, \texttt{discern\_design\_floor}, \texttt{SMCS+retain-incumbent},
\texttt{choe\_ramdas\_eb+front}. Other: QSG, D2, \texttt{economic\_replacement}, \texttt{cams\_adapter},
\texttt{model\_picker\_adapter}, \texttt{CONTROL\_never\_switch}.

\paragraph{Synthetic cohorts.} S-null: F0\_null, F5\_dep\_null\_r8, F5\_dep\_null\_r95,
O\_null\_semimarkov, O\_null\_order2, WP2\_o2\_null, WP2\_sm\_null, WP2\_hs\_null (500 seeds each).
S-alt: F0\_better, F5\_dep\_better\_r8, S\_dep\_better\_r95, WP2\_o2\_better, WP2\_sm\_better,
WP2\_hs\_better (200 seeds each). Seed base 2026110951.

\paragraph{C4 chronological replacement streams.} Chronological split into train-old, train-new,
validation and test. The incumbent is a standardised logistic regression trained on train-old; the
challenger is a HistGradientBoosting classifier trained on train-new, with preprocessing fitted on
each model's own window. Episodes are non-overlapping 2{,}000-event windows of the test period; the
label delay (100 events) is imposed. 27 sources, 2{,}097 episodes; the full table of sources,
episode counts, disagreement rates and content hashes is in \texttt{docs/p2/06\_REAL\_DATA.md}.
% TODO: copy the 27-row table here once the C4 hashes are re-verified at the confirmation run.
Built for an earlier project and not used for any P2-NAD design choice. 3 policy seeds per episode,
seed base 2026110952.

\paragraph{Twins.} \texttt{data/sprint\_twin\_losses} v1: five UCI datasets (metro, beijing\_pm25,
bike\_hour, appliances, household\_power) with exact marginal nulls, 5 policy seeds per episode, seed
base 2026110953. A rebuild (v3) is pending; the confirmation uses v1 as registered.

\paragraph{Development streams.} Five UCI fresh sources (62 episodes) and nine Atlas v2 sources
(168 episodes) were exposed during design and are not confirmation evidence.
