# Source-level theorem-scope audit

Updated 2026-11-04. This is a code-and-assumption review of the local constructions, not an
independent formal proof, peer review, or empirical validity result. The term “coherent” below means
the implementation follows its displayed betting argument when the stated assumptions hold.

Promotion-cycle alpha spending is explicit. | Tests the V1 pointwise conditional null, not the stationary Markov V2 or real twin marginal null. ## Mechanical caveats recorded during review

- Unit tests, null simulations, and low observed promotion rates are evidence about code paths and
  selected generators; none independently proves the supermartingale arguments.
- Log-likelihood floors are a numerical safeguard, not part of the exact mixture proof. A separate
  analytic factor-bound audit establishes non-attainment for the saved T=2000 Sprint 2 runs; this
  is a code-and-horizon bound rather than per-run instrumentation and should be regenerated if the
  factor grid or horizon changes.
- A theorem claim must name its null, filtration, query clock, delay/order assumptions, predictable
  policy state, and repeated-cycle error allocation. The registry's short V labels alone are not a
  complete validity statement.
- SOTA methods in this sprint are source-checked common-contract ports unless the audit explicitly
  identifies a reproduced native core. Their papers do not validate the ShiftLab wrapper, new
  filtration, or added cost/switching rule.
- External/human review remains required for a publication-level proof and comparator audit.
