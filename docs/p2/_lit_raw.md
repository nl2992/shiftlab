# P2 prior-art scan (raw), 2026-11-09
Verification: WebSearch/WebFetch only. "VERIFIED" = title/authors/venue seen on an arXiv/proceedings page or search hit this session. Official code links were NOT found for most items (stated per item rather than guessed).

## Bottom line
No source found that combines (i) VOI label acquisition, (ii) anytime-valid certification, (iii) switching cost / payback, AND (iv) a certificate whose dependence-robustness is declared (rho <= rho_bar) and bought cheaply via spacing/thinning. Closest: DISCERN (i+ii), Karampatziakis et al. gated deployment (ii+deploy), The Challenger (iii, no certificates), Regol et al. (iii, no certificates). The dependence-specific anytime-valid literature looks thin (only one unreviewed mixing preprint found). Searches were not exhaustive; "no hit" is not "does not exist".

## (a) Anytime-valid inference under dependence
1. Ramdas, Grunwald, Vovk, Shafer. "Game-theoretic statistics and safe anytime-valid inference." Statistical Science 2023; arXiv 2210.01948. Survey of e-processes/CS; validity needs a supermartingale-type condition under the null, not iid. Relevance: under a conditional null (conditional mean of increment <= 0 given the past) validity is automatic under dependence; the issue is that this is a stronger hypothesis than a marginal null. Background, no threat.
2. Waudby-Smith, Arbour, Sinha, Kennedy, Ramdas. "Time-uniform central limit theory and asymptotic confidence sequences." Annals of Statistics 52(6):2613-2640, 2024; arXiv 2103.06476. Strong-invariance-principle approximation gives asymptotic CS for iid/martingale data. Template for a HAC-style time-uniform CS; I did not see serial correlation treated (abstract-level reading). Building block, not threat.
3. Yuan, Z. "Self-normalized concentration inequalities of marginal mean with sample variance only." arXiv 2512.01817 (v4 Dec 2025, math.ST). Martingale-difference case uses sample variance; phi/phi-tilde-mixing case uses an empirical block long-run variance. Abstract does not say whether bounds are time-uniform; no code link; single-author, unreviewed. HIGHEST relevance for (a)/(e): a marginal-mean, dependence-robust, block-variance bound is the "dependence-robust certificate" class. If time-uniform, the certificate itself is not our novelty, only its economic use. Must check.
4. Choe & Ramdas. "Combining evidence across filtrations." arXiv 2402.09698 (v5). E-values can be averaged under arbitrary dependence; e-processes w.r.t. different filtrations need care. Governs legitimacy of predictable thinning/blocking (a thinned stream lives on a different filtration). Justification source, not threat.
5. Shin, Ramdas, Rinaldo. "E-detectors: a nonparametric framework for sequential change detection." New England J. Stat. Data Science 2023; arXiv 2203.03532; R package `edetect` on CRAN. Sums of e-processes started at all times; relevant to rollback monitoring. Does not address dependence-robust promotion.
6. Gauthier, Bach, Jordan. "E-values expand the scope of conformal prediction." arXiv 2503.13050. Batch anytime-valid conformal with exchangeability only within batches (blocking-like idea, but for conformal, not paired loss).
7. "Confidence estimation via sequential likelihood mixing." arXiv 2502.14689 (2025). Seen as search hit only; authors/venue NOT verified. Anytime CS for non-iid but well-specified likelihood families; a parametric Markov model of loss differences could use it (competing route).
8. Chugg, Cortes-Gomez, Wilder, Ramdas. "Auditing fairness by betting." NeurIPS 2023; arXiv 2305.17570. Adaptive data collection by a known probabilistic policy plus shift; no serial dependence.
NOT FOUND: Markov-chain-specific e-process / universal inference for Markov chains; Kuchibhotla near-optimal CS and any Whitehouse paper on dependence (Whitehouse, Wu, Ramdas COLT 2025, arXiv 2310.09100, is real but martingale-based); Abeles et al. 2025 (mixing online-to-PAC) seen only second-hand, UNVERIFIED. Shekhar 2025 (arXiv 2512.20039, composite nulls) is iid only.

## (b) Comparing forecasters sequentially
9. Choe & Ramdas. "Comparing sequential forecasters." Operations Research 72(4):1368-1387, 2024; arXiv 2110.00115. CS/e-process for the time-averaged mean score difference, no distributional assumptions on forecasts/outcomes. This is our "cheap certificate valid under a conditional null": it targets the average of conditional means, which explains over-promotion for the marginal quantity under persistence. Baseline, not threat; the contrast is our empirical finding.
10. Henzi & Ziegel. "Valid sequential inference on probability forecast performance." Biometrika 109(3):647-663, 2022; arXiv 2103.08402. E-values for proper-score comparison, valid under optional stopping, with a power cost. Same class as 9.
11. Arnold, Gavrilopoulos, Schulz, Ziegel. "Sequential model confidence sets." arXiv 2404.18678 (v4 Jan 2026; no venue listed). Time-uniform nonasymptotic MCS from e-processes/CS. Multi-model; no costs or dependence robustness per abstract.
12. Giacomini & White. "Tests of conditional predictive ability." Econometrica 74(6):1545-1578, 2006. Fixed-sample HAC-type tests; origin of the conditional vs unconditional distinction. Conceptual ancestor; not sequential.

## (c) Safe deployment, active selection, retraining, switching costs
13. Karampatziakis, Mineiro, Ramdas. "Off-policy confidence sequences." ICML 2021, PMLR 139:5301-5310; arXiv 2102.09540. Likelihood-ratio/betting CS for OPE with an explicit "gated deployment" application (stage candidate, track CS, upgrade when safe). Nearest ancestor of the CS-gate. No label cost, switching cost, or dependence. (I did not confirm the acronym "MOPE" on a primary page.)
14. Xu, Karampatziakis, Mineiro. "Active, anytime-valid risk controlling prediction sets." NeurIPS 2024; arXiv 2406.10490. Label policy chooses per covariate whether to query, model-predicted risk as control variate, anytime-valid under adaptive collection. Overlaps on label acquisition + validity; no switching, no dependence. Disagreement-only labeling is a special case of such policies.
15. Karimi et al., "Online active model selection for pre-trained classifiers" (Model Picker), AISTATS 2021, arXiv 2010.09818 (arXiv ID seen in hits; title/venue from memory, authors not re-verified). Liu, Xia, Stevens, Chen, "Contextual active model selection" (CAMS), NeurIPS 2024, arXiv 2207.06030. Label sparingly for best-model identification/regret; no alpha-level promotion certificate with deployment economics.
16. Balachandran. "Pay only for disagreement: certified no-regression verdicts for model updates with matching label-complexity bounds" (DISCERN). arXiv 2609.17560 (cs.LG). Zero-label tier plus audited tier labeling sampled disagreements with anytime-valid CS; label complexity rho^2/eps^2; one error budget over unbounded promotions; 785 update pairs. Single-author preprint. HIGH THREAT to "pay labels only on disagreement + sequential certificate". Per abstract: no switching cost, delay/payback, or dependence-robustness. Full text NOT read; must check iid assumptions and delay handling.
17. Digalakis Jr, Perignon, Saurin, Sentenac. "The Challenger: when do new data sources justify switching machine learning models?" arXiv 2512.18390. Learning-curve model; optimal evaluation time scales as horizon^(1/(1+alpha)); regret O(T^(2/3) sqrt(log T)). Closest on switching economics + horizon; no statistical certificate or label purchase. Threat only to a "remaining-horizon payback" novelty claim.
18. Regol et al. "When to retrain a machine learning model." ICML 2025 (PMLR 267); arXiv 2505.14903. UPF forecasts performance and retrains when a delta-quantile of retrain cost beats no-retrain; cost ratio alpha. Economic hurdle without certificates. Baseline.
19. Mahadevan & Mathioudakis. "Cost-effective retraining of machine learning models" (CARA), arXiv 2310.04216; Knowledge-Based Systems 2024 (venue from search snippet, title there given as "Cost-aware retraining for machine learning"). Staleness cost vs retrain cost. Baseline.
20. Katz. "Cost-sensitive retraining via posterior learning debt." arXiv 2604.06438. Synthetic proof of concept, retrain when expected regret exceeds cost. Minor.
21. Fernandez-Barrios, Pastor-Lopez, Pikatza-Huerga, Garcia Bringas. "Candidate comparability before promotion: conditional validation in adaptive network intrusion detection." arXiv 2609.04388 (cs.CR). Empirical study: drift alarm does not justify promotion; policy rankings unstable. No certificates; usable as empirical caution.
22. Switching costs: Geulen, Voecking, Winkler (Shrinking Dartboard, COLT 2010; from search snippet only); Kalai & Vempala 2005 (follow-the-lazy-leader); Dekel, Ding, Koren, Peres, "Bandits with switching costs: T^(2/3) regret," STOC 2014, arXiv 1310.2997; Sherman & Koren "Lazy OCO," arXiv 2102.03803. Adversarial-regret view of switching, no alpha gating. Zliobaite not searched; unverified.

## (d) Combined VOI + certification + switching; selecting among e-processes
No paper found doing all three. Pairwise overlaps only: DISCERN and Xu et al. (acquisition + certificate), Karampatziakis (certificate + deployment), Challenger/Regol/CARA (switching/timing). On multiple e-processes: only background (Choe-Ramdas combining evidence; Wasserman-Ramdas-Balakrishnan "Universal inference," PNAS 2020, arXiv 1912.11436, hit only). Adaptive choice of null/certificate level (cheap vs robust) from data was found in no source. That is where a novelty claim could live, but validity of data-dependent switching between certificates needs a predictable-selection argument or alpha-split/mixture; no existing treatment found.

## (e) Cost/power of dependence-robust sequential tests
Only 2512.01817 (block long-run variance under mixing) and generic remarks that e-value tests lose power (Henzi-Ziegel). Nothing found quantifying a ~12% total-cost price or adapting robustness to estimated dependence (adaptive block length, estimated long-run variance in a CS). Not verified absent.

## Threat assessment
- Likely novel (as far as searched): certificate declared under Markov rho <= rho_bar marginal null; spacing queries beyond correlation time / predictable thinning as a cheap route to robustness; coupling with payback hurdle; the empirical over-promotion of cheap CS under persistence.
- Not novel (cite, do not claim): disagreement-only labeling + anytime CS (DISCERN, Xu et al.); CS-gated deployment (Karampatziakis 2021); horizon-based switching (Challenger); cost-ratio retraining (Regol, CARA); paired-forecaster CS (Choe-Ramdas).
- Must check before submission (not done): DISCERN full text; time-uniformity of 2512.01817; filtration conditions in Choe-Ramdas for thinned streams; primary source for delayed-label filtrations (delay ~100 makes the filtration lag).
- Caution on spacing: thinning by gap g under Markov rho leaves residual dependence ~rho^g, never exactly independent; validity needs an explicit bound (mixing-based correction), not only an estimated correlation time. No source found supplying that bound.
