# Research charter (h2c-restart) — branch `research/h2c-restart-risk-certified-2026-10-30`, from `60930a417952b3059b6309fa519a1abb4af893fb`
Historical verdicts (HG, H2c, G1–G5 confirmations) are final under their own protocols; the legacy exit condition stays unmet and unedited. CI repair: unused `xs`/`xt` in `generators.g5_effects` renamed (`_xs`, `_xt`), draws unchanged.
**Question.** Can H2c's Brier-guided cost evidence be combined with valid restarted evidence to beat the strongest *finite-horizon-valid* cost-null comparator, and is the existing cost SR's extra power a restart effect or a threshold artefact?
**Validity vocabulary (kept separate throughout):** ARL (E_{H0}[τ] ≥ 1/α) vs finite-horizon error (P_{H0}(τ ≤ T) ≤ α). Mode B (matched finite-horizon error at α = .05, T = 2000, theory thresholds) is the primary comparison mode; Mode C (empirical thresholds) is reported separately.
**Development protocol (written before running):** fresh seeds 100000+, 400 seeds per world, G1 deployed model, concept-shift alternatives δ ∈ {1.5, 2} × onset {300, 1000, 1500} × persistence {abrupt, transient (length 200), recurrent (period 400)} + one null world; label rates 5%, 10%, 20%; delay 5. Methods: restart-weight families {single (= ordinary), uniform, front, geometric, coarse8} applied to factors from H2c, cost-only ONS (R3), Brier-guided ONS (R4); SR-0 (existing `sr_cost_tight`, empirical threshold, Mode C), SR-1 (shared-factor SR recursion with uniform cap) at the ARL threshold 1/α and at the finite-horizon threshold T/α. Metrics: power (alarm ≥ first harmful step), null false alarm over the horizon, mean delay, accumulated harm before alarm. Selection rule for finalists: (i) null FA ≤ .07 at the theory threshold, and (ii) mean power over the late/transient/recurrent cells exceeds the best single-start comparator by ≥ 0.03 at ≥ 2 of the 3 label rates. Not run in this development pass: R2 (Brier-guided capital allocation), R5 (multi-horizon), R6 (budget-aware restart), nominal-risk uncertainty, dynamic proxy stress; they are listed as open.
