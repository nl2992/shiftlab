## What “end” means for this objective

The initial implementation and controlled-benchmark milestone is largely complete; that narrower milestone may reasonably have been described as roughly 85–90% done. That percentage does not describe the full research objective or paper. EIA and Upworthy meet the two-task-family count at the level of frozen component evaluations, but do not jointly validate a unified monitor–certify–intervene system. The working manuscript scope is now a retrospective benchmark/failure analysis across Q1–Q3; publication-level differentiation is still unverified. The remaining critical path is: (1) finish the source-by-source literature and task-compatible comparator review; (2) verify that real-data experiments meet the charter's requirement to execute and compare actual monitoring, selection, or intervention logic, and fill any remaining gaps; (3) reconcile claims, endpoints, tables and figures against verified artifacts; and (4) run final tests/verifiers, complete review, and freeze the manuscript and code in a reviewed Git revision. Several focused weeks are a planning estimate for this bounded manuscript, not a commitment; the full objective has no defensible calendar end date until those gates close. A claim of end-to-end real-data benefit remains unsupported and would require a compatible data contract and further confirmation.

The next work should close the finite comparator-applicability audit and establish a valid real-data task contract before any final evaluation. **Criteo Uplift v2.1 has been ruled out for causal policy-value evaluation under the documented release process:** its paper confirms non-uniform negative-label sampling but publishes no inclusion probabilities. The Criteo BLBF archive remains a possible logged-policy source but is approximately 35 GB compressed, non-click impressions are subsampled, and no standard redistribution license is stated. CUVET-Policy's source integrity and train coverage are verified, but its full-train arm means violate CUVET's response ordering; the paper's bucket aggregation definition confirms that count-weighted raw sums do not resolve this mismatch. Faithful CUVET fitting is stopped on this release absent source clarification. Its official test remains unread, exact assignment probabilities and protocol freeze remain open, and flexible policy evaluation has not been run. Neither CUVET nor Hillstrom supplies the sequential timestamps needed for monitoring. Project GATE's public-use archives are locally acquired and aggregate schema-audited: the DOL final report explains the 4,198-to-4,197 cohort change as removal of one nonrandomly assigned applicant. The final impact analysis also excluded 120 applicants in mixed-arm business partnerships (analysis n=4,078), but the public files contain no partnership IDs or individual analysis weights.
