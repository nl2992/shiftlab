# Delayed evidence-directed PPI: disjoint harm confirmation results

**Confirmation gate: PASS.** On the frozen, disjoint seed block 20282000–20282999, the calibrated candidate improved post-onset detection over uniform and used fewer labels under the same 480-label request cap.

The 1,000 paired boundary-harm streams used the frozen source-only proxy, 2,400-event horizon, onset at event 1,200, harm mean shift 0.20, `epsilon=0.05`, independent delays uniform on 0–12, and identical query uniforms/outcomes/delays within each seed. The candidate was `evidence_a0p025_p0p5_eventwise`; the comparator was uniform PPI at q=0.20. The confirmation runner verified the passing calibration manifest, matching development-code hash, and disjoint seed blocks before execution.

The candidate alarmed after onset on **92/1,000 streams (9.2%)**, compared with **36/1,000 (3.6%)** for uniform. There were 76 candidate-only detections and 20 uniform-only detections. The paired detection gain was **+5.6 percentage points** (paired bootstrap 95% interval **[+3.7, +7.5] points**); the predeclared one-sided exact McNemar test gave **p=3.66×10⁻⁹**. Both methods had four pre-onset alarms; those streams count as non-detections in the primary endpoint.

The candidate queried a mean of **158.77 labels** per stream versus **467.42** for uniform, a paired difference of **−308.66 labels** (descriptive 95% bootstrap interval **[−312.73, −304.46]**). Neither method exceeded the cap; uniform used all 480 labels on 472 streams, while the candidate never exhausted it. Candidate streams processed 2,354.1 events on average versus 2,341.7 for uniform. Mean event-time delay among post-onset detections was 778.7 events for the candidate and 803.7 for uniform; this delay comparison is secondary and conditioned on detection.

All 2,000 method-stream rows passed uniqueness, hard-cap, and resolved-prefix audits; no pending label remained. The paired significance and efficiency result supports this candidate on the frozen IID synthetic DGP after separate null calibration. It does not establish general superiority, expected harm reduction in deployment, robustness to serial dependence or informative feedback, or novelty. The query mechanism and bounded-mean e-process are established constructions; the studied contribution here is a decision-cost application and an empirical delayed-feedback comparison.
