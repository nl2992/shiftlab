# Manuscript-ready four-contribution outline

## Proposed paper framing

**Working title:** When Label Budgets Hide Recovery: A Reproducible Failure Atlas for Adaptive
Deployment Under Delayed Feedback.

This framing is a benchmark/case-study paper unless a genuinely distinct repair survives the bounded
pilot and prospective confirmation. Do not claim a new algorithm from a known reserve, pacing,
rollback or detector composition.

### Contribution 1 — Diagnosis (PARTIAL)

Document reproducible late-improvement blindness and reversal/rollback sensitivity in adaptive
deployment with explicit finite pathwise label budgets, delayed feedback and switch costs. Show
regime-level examples, ordinary null and dependence stresses, and compare against strong baselines.
Scope the findings to ShiftLab's simulator/constructed replay contracts. Existing corrected suites
are reproducible correction replays, not a new independent benchmark validation.

### Contribution 2 — Mechanism (PARTIAL)

Use controlled changes to distinguish evidence timing, acquisition spending, rollback thresholds,
restart-grid behavior and reserve effects. Include negative controls and source-level cost
decomposition. Current evidence indicates some apparent benefits are explained by front-loading,
alpha tuning, or fewer labels; no novel interaction is established. Report pilot results and
confounding faithfully.

### Contribution 3 — Repair (FAIL unless pilot overturns)

State the explicit repair only if it remains distinct after matched E_rb tuning, two-sided SR/CUSUM,
fixed reserve and simple front-loading. Audit originality against primary sources. Evaluate scoped
net deployment cost, predictive loss, query expenditure, switching expenditure, promotion latency
and correctly specified null error. No unsupported guarantee or universal SOTA claim. At present
the novelty audit does not identify a distinct mechanism.

### Contribution 4 — Validation (FAIL)

Require fresh synthetic confirmation and independent chronological real sources with authentic
training, prediction and label-release chronology. Analyze paired outcomes by source cluster and
include adverse regimes. Existing exposed, constructed real streams are descriptive only; Wild-Time
label-release chronology is unverified and OPSD/Binance holdouts remain locked.

## Figures and publication assets

Use validated output values for plots and preserve source tables. PaperBanana is not available in
the current tool environment; research figures must wait for that dependency rather than silently
substitute a different generator. Current artifacts are primarily tables and source records.
