\section{Experiments}\label{sec:experiments}

% Confirmation numbers come from docs/p2/07_CONFIRMATION.md (RESULTS) and docs/p2/results/confirm.md.

\paragraph{Protocol.} Arms, panel, cohorts and decision rules were registered before any
confirmation run (\texttt{docs/p2/07\_CONFIRMATION.md}). Arm A is NAD-2S (\texttt{NAD2S-g10-f6}),
claimed class $\mathcal M(.8)$. Arm B is the payback variant \texttt{PB80-g10}. NAD-m80-g10 is
descriptive. Directional tests use 99\% percentile intervals from 4{,}000 bootstrap draws, by seed
(stratified by regime) on synthetic data and by (source, episode) cluster with source-equal weights on
C4.

\paragraph{Panel (20 policies).} Arms A, B and NAD-m80-g10; dependence-robust comparators A5b, D5 and
QSG-env8; published-certificate ports (author-endpoint MOPE: \texttt{gated\_deployment\_native},
\texttt{gated\_adaptive\_range\_native}, and the latter with front-loading; quad-endpoint MOPE
\texttt{gated\_adaptive\_range}; DISCERN-style, SMCS with retain-incumbent, Choe--Ramdas EB with
front-loading); and QSG, D2, \texttt{economic\_replacement}, CAMS and Model Picker adapters and
never-switch. Ports are common-contract re-implementations, not author code, except the
\texttt{*\_native} MOPE rows, whose endpoint follows the authors' released code
(\cref{app:impl}); there is no public DISCERN code, so that row is an approximation.

\paragraph{Cohorts.} \textbf{S-null}: eight null regimes, 500 seeds each. \textbf{S-alt}: six
alternative regimes, 200 seeds each. \textbf{C4}: 27 chronological replacement streams, 2{,}097
episodes, never run in P2 before the freeze, with imposed delay 100; they are weakly dependent
(lag-1 autocorrelation of the disagreement subsequence $\le.23$) so they test whether robustness
gives up too much when dependence is weak, not validity under strong dependence. \textbf{Twin}: five
UCI datasets with exact marginal nulls. Full descriptions are in \cref{app:panel}.

\paragraph{Negative results.} Four of seven registered hypotheses failed. H2 and H3: on the 27 weakly
dependent C4 streams NAD-2S ties A5b ($+0.23$, $+0.1\%$) and D5 ($+0.14$), so the roughly $3\%$
synthetic advantage does not transfer to real replacement streams. H6: the payback arm B reaches a
max 95\% upper bound of mean decision harm of $1.47>1.0$ on F5\_dep\_null\_r95, which lies outside its
claimed class $\mathcal M(.8)$. H7: B is not cheaper than A5b ($-0.32$, 99\% CI $[-3.21,+2.59]$).

promote at $.418$ and $.438$ on the hidden-state null, as does A ($.366$); that null is outside every class.

\paragraph{Development finding on the author-endpoint MOPE comparator.} In development
\devnote\ the author-endpoint adaptive-range gate (\texttt{gated\_adaptive\_range\_native}) is cheaper
than the NAD candidates but promotes on 15--35\% of persistent nulls (development; confirmation: $.316$ on r95), whereas the quad-endpoint port
is more conservative (it also had a wider interval than the authors' code in a parity check
\devnote). This gate claims a V1 null, so the promotions are outside its stated guarantee rather than
a violated theorem. We report both because the quad-endpoint port would otherwise understate the gap in cost.

\paragraph{Development context (not confirmation).} On 40 selection seeds \devnote, the cheapest
certificate with in-class null promotion $\le.025$ was NAD-m80-g10, 2--4\% cheaper than the
quad-endpoint MOPE port and A5b-type comparators and 6--12\% more expensive than V1 front-loaders that
promote on 25--70\% of persistent nulls. The 14 development streams are exposed, and the design
choices were made with them in view.

\subsection{Robustness value}

The anytime robustness value is recorded only by NAD-m80-g10. It is implemented with a validity
argument (\cref{prop:rv}) but was not evaluated as a decision aid in the registered confirmation, and
the confirmation outputs report no distribution of $\rhob^*$; we make no empirical claim about it.
