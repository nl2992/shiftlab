# H2c G5 comparator selection on stored seeds (partial W03.06)

The inherited G5 analysis selects the better of C1 discounted Kelly and C2 cost ONS separately in each harm cell using their realised 600-seed means. Its 4,000-draw bootstrap then holds each selected comparator fixed. The original analysis script reruns on the archived parquet and cell CSV and reproduces D1 at 10%: +0.086 [0.059, 0.113], and at 20%: +0.094 [0.063, 0.125]. Both stored files match their registered SHA-256 hashes: parquet `ed5923e9486c6bc9c8a0d1497f68c74563a4221f5644721f508dfd7fbbf66ba7` and cell CSV `81c8e39d6afc29ce88a62a28d42395d11cc3661af2ff538e0978c0e04c1877b6`.

`scripts/retrospective_audit/h2c_disjoint_comparator_selection.py` uses the same exposed data for a *post hoc* two-fold diagnostic: for each of 14 harm cells and each fraction, select C1/C2 on seeds 0–299 and compare on 300–599, then reverse train/test. Every seed is evaluated once against a comparator selected on the disjoint half. The full-data selected effects recompute as +0.085833 and +0.093810; the disjoint-selection effects are +0.090000 and +0.095119. Five of 28 cell/fraction rows choose different comparators across halves. The full-data selected comparator agrees with at least one half in every row. Exact cell choices and effects are in `H2C_DISJOINT_SELECTION.json`.

The point estimates do not show an upward artifact from the original comparator choice; selecting the strongest realised baseline on the same seeds is conservative for the candidate's *descriptive* difference among these two arms. This diagnostic does not deliver a selection-adjusted interval, a new preregistered comparator, independent confirmation, source-faithful published baseline reproduction, or generator-level validity. The original G5 classification remains inherited, with its caveats. Reviewer: lead, INTERNAL_ONLY; G03 remains unsigned.
