## Cross-claim restrictions

- Never pool detection, predictive risk, decision cost, CTR, forecast error, label use, update counts or imposed intervention costs into a single score without a justified common unit and estimand.
- Never treat synthetic evaluator-only oracle outcomes as learner inputs or as an implementable policy.
- Distinguish finite-sample theorem assumptions from Monte Carlo calibration evidence; zero observed errors in a finite simulation is not proof of validity.
- Distinguish task-family count from a unified-system evaluation. EIA plus Upworthy satisfy two component-task families; neither executes the full system.
- Label every EIA/CARA/RCCDA/UPF/ADWIN/DDM transfer as an adaptation where the paper's original task and data contract differ.
- Preserve negative findings, invalid historical runs and exposed holdouts in the provenance/limitations record; they cannot be silently removed from the research history.
