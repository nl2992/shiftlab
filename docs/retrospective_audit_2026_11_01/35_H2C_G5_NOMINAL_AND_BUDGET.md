# H2c G5 nominal-risk and query-budget scope (partial W03/W04)

The G5 runner gives F1 H2c, C1 cost discounted and C2 cost ONS the same Bernoulli label rate and realised mask within each cell. It does **not** implement a pathwise hard query cap. `scripts/retrospective_audit/audit_g5_query_budget.py` reconstructs the actual `label_mask` RNG draws from the runner's `lseed=95000+k` mapping (where `k` is incremented before the arm loop). Across 32 cells × 600 paths, at frac .10, 9,246/19,200 paths query more than the nominal 200 of 2,000 events; counts range 151–260 with mean 200.022. At frac .20, 9,447/19,200 query more than 400; counts range 325–475 with mean 400.135. The reconstruction was checked against `detectors.label_mask` for the first cell. These numbers are exact for the seeded masks, not a simulation of alarms. They do not contradict the G5 protocol, which registered *label rates*; they preclude describing the G5 result as evidence under a hard B=200/400 cap. The separate historical `docs/h2c_next/HARD_BUDGET_AUDIT.md` reports sharply lower power when a cap is imposed and the bet stops after exhaustion, but those results were not independently replayed here.

The G5 cost-null increment subtracts `source_moments("G5:<condition>")["loss"][0]` as its nominal cost. `source_moments` generates 100 no-shift source paths of length 4,000 from the *same known G5 generator* at seed 999 and uses their sample mean directly. The frozen `_pp_parts` had no `r0_cost` upper-bound override, and `simulate_g5` does not attach an upper bound. A later source version added an optional override, but that was not part of G5. This is 400,000 source observations per condition, making the reference numerically precise in this synthetic design, but it is still an estimated mean with no confidence allowance in the subtracted value. The source-code argument for a conditional cost-null e-process requires a valid nominal upper reference; this audit has not established unconditional Type-I control when that sample mean undershoots the true nominal risk. It also does not turn the source generator's known condition into a deployable nominal-risk estimation procedure.

Thus the registered G5 D1 advantage remains a stored-seed result under expected-rate Bernoulli feedback and an effectively known synthetic nominal reference. It does not certify hard-budget operation or unknown-nominal validity. The frozen result and its protocol remain unchanged.
