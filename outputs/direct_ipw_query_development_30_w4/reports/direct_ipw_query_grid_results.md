## Equal-weight alternative macro contrasts

Each contrast is candidate minus uniform, averaged equally over the registered harmful scenarios within each seed and then bootstrapped by seed cluster (10,000 resamples). Positive detection and exposure avoided are favorable; negative labels and spend indicate resource savings. Intervals are descriptive, percentile-based, and unadjusted across this development grid.

`excess_cost_avoided` credits evaluator-known avoided excess cost when fallback occurs after an alarm or when the hard-budget monitor stops. It can therefore reflect both earlier fallback and statistical escalation; detection, processed events, label counts, and spend are reported separately. Synthetic tariffs and decision-cost units are not combined into a single utility without an explicit conversion parameter.
