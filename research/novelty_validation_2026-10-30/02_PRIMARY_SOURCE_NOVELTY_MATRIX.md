## Reduction of the H2c claim (Gate A)

H2c = `{ per-event PP-IPW increment [rows 2, 4] } × { predictable discounted-Kelly bet [predictable betting; WSR-style] } × { bet fraction chosen from another statistic's history [side-information/mixture device] } × { α-split nominal risk [row 1] }`.

* Exact reduction to its closest prior algorithm: `pp_cost_guided(mode="guided_disc")` becomes plain cost-only discounted Kelly when the guiding statistic is replaced by the cost statistic (verified numerically, max |Δ log-wealth| = 0 to 9e-16, `tests::test_h2c_reduces…`).
* Validity does not depend on the guide: any predictable λ_t ∈ [0, cap] gives a nonnegative supermartingale under the null ⇒ H2c's validity is the generic betting argument, not a new result.
* No theorem of growth-rate improvement exists (repo: CONJECTURED); a Kelly-restatement would not qualify (task rule).

**Gate A verdict: H2c is a COMPOSITION of known techniques with no independently defensible theorem.** Retained as a benchmark component; the novelty claim is **unsubstantiated** and is withdrawn. Proceed to the task's C1 analysis only to characterise the mechanism, then C2.
