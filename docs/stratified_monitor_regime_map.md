## Matched comparisons and claims

Every candidate and incumbent receives the same applicable gating, correct bounds, label-dollar
cap, query uniforms, proxy access, and betting-engine choices. The historic five-stake engine
remains for fidelity and WP1's registered growth endpoint. A grid extended to stake 1 and
predictable adaptive engines must also be given to every compatible arm before claiming a win.
Engine choices and multiplicity accounting are fixed in each protocol, not selected using the
evaluation block. Full-feedback references are explicitly unmatched upper references.

Keep weighted-Bonferroni intersection-null results next to pooled-null results, with the null in
every leaderboard row. Same-null Chugg-style compositions receive local bounds and the same
allocations too: relabelling that composition as our method cannot produce a method claim.
Allocation ablations use identical strata, engines, coins, and caps. Matched-bound ablations use
identical increments and labels. Agreement skipping is credited to DISCERN.

The candidate's primary development screen requires at least +10 detection points or 1.5 times
lower censored delay at equal or fewer labels against the strongest applicable incumbent;
other registered cells require no loss beyond 3 points. Candidate protocols retain any stricter
phase-goal gate. Calibration requires the registered simultaneous exact upper bound at most 5%.
This phase stops before confirmation: it may nominate a design, never record a confirmed win.

Classify every reported cell as dominates, regime-specific win, tie, or loss only within its
evidence stage. No primary development win means tie or loss; a development win remains a screen.
List excluded methods and why. Comparecast targets a weak average and PPRM a predictive risk;
their results cannot establish a same-null decision-harm win. Q2 promotion methods are outside
this monitoring phase. Feature sentinels are diagnostics, not harm evidence.
