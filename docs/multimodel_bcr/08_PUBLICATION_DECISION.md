# Publication decision for the BCR pivot

**INCOMPLETE. The new-algorithm (BCR) programme stops at the feasibility gate.** Per directive
§17, work returns to the existing EQSDp methods-and-analysis paper and its registered C4.

The K-model pilot adds three development findings to the analysis track (03_PILOT_RESULTS.md,
K-model findings):
- churn from active selection without an economic hurdle;
- multiple-comparison churn that grows with K;
- label efficiency of Model-Picker acquisition at equal net cost.

It also extends the main analytical message to K=3–5: a tuned confidence-bound payback switcher
is at the empirical frontier, and budget-saving candidate elimination only trades labels for
recovery delay.
