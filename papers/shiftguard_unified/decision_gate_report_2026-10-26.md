# ShiftGuard research decision gate — 2026-10-26

## Decision

**Do not develop RIVE, ASC, or another new algorithm in this iteration.** The frozen integrated benchmark does not show a general advantage over simpler systems, and the available real-data tasks do not provide the independent, fully specified feedback and downstream-utility contract needed to justify a targeted intervention method. This is a research decision, not a claim that the overall programme or manuscript is complete. A frozen Open Bandit position-1 MOPE replay has since completed as retrospective exploratory analysis; its confidence set became empty at event 866 and neither MOPE nor fixed-horizon Hoeffding promoted. This adds a method-level diagnostic, not validity-bearing OBD evidence.

The implementation and controlled-benchmark milestone is substantially complete. The latest full local test run reports **1,566 passing tests, one skip, and zero failures in 629.95 seconds**; Ruff, formatting, configured mypy, and `git diff --check` pass in the current worktree. The RTX 4060 CUDA path is usable, and multiple registered synthetic confirmations have independent verification. The frozen Upworthy Q2 task and the EIA 2026 repeat both passed their scoped reproducibility checks, but these checks establish software and bounded experiment progress; they do not show integrated real-data efficacy or complete publication evidence. The Open Bandit replay verifier also passed on the current working tree, matching the candidate, event path, and all recorded hashes; its empty-set outcome is exploratory and assumption-limited.

## Evidence that determines the decision

1. **Integrated efficacy is profile-specific.** In the frozen 100-seed-per-profile comparison, the full system improved on continuing under two monitor-aligned harm profiles, but simpler two-gate and fixed-candidate methods had higher utility in those same profiles. The integrated system missed native late-onset harm, performed poorly after a post-promotion reversal, and its controller ablation produced the same decisions. The frozen comparison is complete and independently verified; do not rerun it without a new registered question. See [`integrated_efficacy_confirmation_results_v1.md`](integrated_efficacy_confirmation_results_v1.md).
2. **The strongest component results are conditional.** Comparecast, PPE/PPRM, Target-Scout, fixed-start mixtures, and intervention components have registered synthetic results, but they test different nulls, feedback assumptions, DGPs, and objectives. Target-Scout's confirmed advantage is limited to its registered IID synthetic workflow. No result establishes a general unified-method advantage. See [`evidence_inventory.md`](evidence_inventory.md) and [`gap_analysis.md`](gap_analysis.md).
3. **Two task-specific final-period real-data evaluations now exist; the integrated real-data system remains untested.** The EIA 2026 frozen replay compares update schedules and forecast risk under assumed label delays; a separate CUDA replay exactly reproduced the aggregate metrics, monthly table, block intervals, and report. Upworthy's frozen selector exceeded uniform package choice by 0.1002 CTR percentage points on 3,808 eligible holdout tests (95% week-cluster interval 0.0850–0.1170 points), and a separate verifier reconstructed the full policy evaluation. Together these distinct families test retraining-resource allocation and an actual package-choice outcome, meeting the task-family count at the component level. EIA source release times and operational utility are unknown; Upworthy's public split is project-relative, lacks the publisher's 2024 problem flag, and supports CTR only. Neither evaluates the complete monitor–certify–intervene system or supplies prospective validity. Other limits remain: CRSP has an exposed test period and unknown source availability/costs; WILDS and Gas Sensor are offline domain tasks; Criteo feedback maturity is inadequate for its proposed within-stream monitor; Hillstrom's evaluation split is exposed; Project GATE has differential follow-up, missing partnership identifiers/weights, and unknown label-release time. See [`real_data_confirmation_gate_audit.md`](real_data_confirmation_gate_audit.md), [`real_data_inventory.md`](real_data_inventory.md), [`upworthy_real_data_eligibility_audit.md`](upworthy_real_data_eligibility_audit.md), and [`real_data_development_results.md`](real_data_development_results.md).
4. **Comparator closure is scope-dependent.** The finite audit identifies which methods have task-compatible comparisons and which require logged actions, propensities, safe-reference outcomes, or a different estimand. AnyRank's corrected pilot is inconclusive and only a star-shaped adaptation. OCS/HCPI/CPC/CPS/CABAI are not valid comparisons on the current common-label tasks. UPF/RCCDA/CARA/ADWIN/DDM have task-specific EIA adaptations, not published-task reproductions. The ranked inclusion and exclusion decision for the present benchmark is now explicit in [`comparator_scope_decision_v1.md`](comparator_scope_decision_v1.md); it does not make the existing task-specific adaptations a comprehensive SOTA frontier. See [`comparator_applicability_audit_v1.md`](comparator_applicability_audit_v1.md) and [`sota_fidelity_matrix.md`](sota_fidelity_matrix.md).

## Scope recommendation

For the next research milestone, treat this work as a **methods and benchmark audit**, not as evidence that a unified intervention system is ready for deployment. Preserve the negative integrated result. Two real-data families now provide component-level external checks, but they do not support a unified real-world intervention claim. The selected working manuscript is a retrospective benchmark/failure analysis across Q1–Q3; its publication-level differentiation remains unverified. The full research objective still requires charter-aligned real-data execution and the remaining comparator, claim, and manuscript gates. Any claim of end-to-end real-world intervention still requires a compatible data contract and has no reliable calendar estimate.

The immediate method go/no-go gate is closed: **NO-GO for new algorithm development.** The active path is to complete the selected retrospective benchmark/failure-analysis manuscript and close the remaining charter-aligned evidence gates. Acquire additional data only if it can answer a registered question with an eligible comparator, primary metric, resource budget, and stopping rule.

**Novelty scope update (2026-10-26):** a targeted primary-source refresh added Digalakis et al.'s sequential statistical/economic incumbent–challenger switching framework (arXiv:2512.18390v2) and Löfström's cost-ordered conformal repair/refit rule (PMLR 329, 2026), alongside Regol et al.'s ICML 2025 cost-aware retraining method. These works substantially cover the broad transition from evidence and resource costs to model switching or intervention. ShiftGuard has not demonstrated a distinct guarantee, estimand, or general advantage beyond them. Therefore the phrase “statistically justified transition from evidence to costed intervention” is not a defensible novelty claim on current evidence. A benchmark/failure-analysis contribution remains possible but is not yet shown to be publication-level distinctive; the literature refresh was targeted, not exhaustive. The Rahman–Tabassum source text also contains inconsistent descriptions of threshold provenance and DTRC parameters, and its selected frontier is retrospective; see [`temporal_reliability_cost_control_audit.md`](temporal_reliability_cost_control_audit.md). Details and primary references are in [`novelty_audit.md`](novelty_audit.md).

The ranked comparator scope decision is now recorded in [`comparator_scope_decision_v1.md`](comparator_scope_decision_v1.md), and candidate manuscript claims are mapped to protocols and verified outputs in [`claim_evidence_map_v1.md`](claim_evidence_map_v1.md). A claim-bounded text draft is assembled in [`manuscript_draft_v0.md`](manuscript_draft_v0.md). These close evidence-triage and initial-drafting tasks only. The working manuscript scope is selected as a retrospective benchmark/failure analysis; its publication-level contribution, reference and figure review, required real-data coverage, and submission-ready manuscript remain open.
