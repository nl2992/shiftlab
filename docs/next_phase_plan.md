## Phase C: compare against closest established methods

The first theorem-level audit confirms that PPRM is the closest directly relevant risk-monitoring baseline: it already combines labeled residual correction, unlabeled predictions, predictable proxy reliance, and an anytime-valid running-risk lower confidence sequence. Its source-risk estimand differs from ShiftLab's paired current-environment policy-cost contrast, and it does not certify a replacement policy. This adapts PPRM's running-risk bound to paired costs and omits its separate source-risk upper confidence bound. The adaptive-η block monitor is integrated into the hard-budget runner. The matched independent 200-stream comparison and the separate 2,000-stream registered-null calibration are complete and documented in [`pprm_paired_decision_benchmark.md`](pprm_paired_decision_benchmark.md), including the residual-variance harm reclassification. In those tested designs, block-PPI estimation can be useful, but the 10% fixed mixture did not match the eventwise monitor's harm-detection power.
