# WP3 comparator and resource scope (partial W03.04)

The historical WP3 20-seed prescreen remains a **failed advancement screen**. At the registered 75%-onset, 5%-budget cell, `optional_candidate/fixed1/sr` detected 11/20 paths, had mean censored delay 488.35, used 48.05 labels and $71.4975. The same-null `optional_temporal/fixed1/started` arm detected 20/20, had mean censored delay 172.20, used 110.40 labels and $165.5949. Thus the candidate spent $94.0975 less and used 62.35 fewer labels on average, while detecting 9 fewer of the 20 paired paths. Its cost is about 43.18% of the temporal arm's cost. Neither arm's single-cell record is a calibrated validity or independent power confirmation.

I recomputed these aggregate measures from the 40 ignored local `outputs/data_efficient_prescreen_20/path_*.csv` files in the original worktree. `scripts/retrospective_audit/recompute_wp3.py` checked every raw file against the run manifest, counted 39,200 rows, verified the exact 20-seed block in each of 1,960 arm/cell groups, recomputed detection/delay from alarm and onset, checked finite hard budgets, and compared all 1,960 group aggregates to `analysis/summary.csv` with zero mismatches. The compact result retains the 16 rows for the two highlighted arms in `WP3_PATH_RECOMPUTATION.json`. The archived summary SHA-256 is `1da55ce007459de3cbb4632c42b1e1810a173db1a47c3703012cd1f2f8f2ca28`. The historical `decision.json` (SHA-256 `cb4248b2be6db78225fed918e84f65bbd95d5fdafba7e6b7f8329379ad273d50`) explicitly records `calibration_established: false`, `confirmation_claim: false`, 208 primary comparators, and `all_comparisons_pass: false`. This checks the archived path records and aggregate arithmetic; it does not regenerate the source paths, replay the monitoring algorithm from individual events, or independently verify comparator registration.

The candidate and temporal arm share the registered harm-null comparison and a mean $180.0327 cap in this cell. They use different fractions of that allowance, so detection and resource use must be shown together. The full-feedback reference has different access to labels. Feature ADWIN targets feature shift. Neither is a same-information harm comparator. The historical prescreen write-up already makes those distinctions and already says to stop WP3 before development, null calibration, real-data testing, or confirmation. No replacement threshold or new WP3 claim is proposed here.

An independent second pass, `scripts/retrospective_audit/audit_wp3_paired.py`, reconstructed all 1,696 eligible same-null paired comparisons from the path rows and matched the archived numeric differences and gate flags with zero mismatches (`WP3_PAIRED_COMPARATOR_AUDIT.json`). In the primary cell the candidate passes the historical rule against 160/208 comparators and fails against 48/208, so the registered *all-comparators* gate fails. Five primary comparators detect 20/20; the temporal fixed1/started arm has the shortest mean censored delay among those five. This is an observed selection on the exposed 20 paths, not a preselected independent test. For that selected pair, nine seed differences are -1 and eleven are zero; the exact empirical paired-bootstrap percentile limits are [-65,-25] percentage points, matching the historical 10,000-draw interval. The interval conditions on that pair and does not account for selecting it from the comparator family.
