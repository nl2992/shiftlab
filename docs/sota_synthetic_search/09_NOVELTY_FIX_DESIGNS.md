# Targeted fix designs (frozen before running)

All fixes act on the strong valid baselines `pprm_tight` (Brier risk) and `pp_cost_tight` (decision cost) and keep the supermartingale argument: each changes only the **predictable** bet or the **predictable** query probability, never the increment's centering, and the one-sided cap `0.5/|X_min|` is retained.

## DK — discounted predictable Kelly bet
1. **Parent:** `pprm_tight`, `pp_cost_tight`. 2. **Change:** replace the full-history running moments in the Kelly bet by exponentially discounted moments with factor γ. 3. **Mechanism targeted:** F3/F2, bet adaptation lag after a change. 4. **Predicted direction:** shorter delay, higher power on abrupt and transient shifts, more variance in the bet (possible loss on gradual or weak shifts). 5. **Matched baseline:** γ = 1 (identical to the parent). 6. **Primary metric:** detection probability on harm worlds at null false alarm ≤ 0.07. 7. **Cost:** none. 8. **Should fail if:** the lag is not from bet adaptation (bets already near the cap), or if discounting inflates variance enough to lose power on gradual shifts. 9. **Falsification experiment:** γ ∈ {0.995, 0.99, 0.98} vs γ = 1 on the same configurations.

## HG — decision-cost-gated predictive-risk e-process
1. **Parent:** `pprm_tight` and `pp_cost_tight` (hybrid). 2. **Change:** the Brier bet is set to zero unless the discounted mean of the decision-cost increment (same labels) is positive; window w ∈ {100, 200}. 3. **Mechanism targeted:** F4. 4. **Predicted direction:** keeps most of the Brier power on harm worlds while suppressing false alarms where predictive loss rises and decision cost falls (`prior`). 5. **Matched baseline:** `pprm_tight`, and `pp_cost_tight` for the false-alarm side. 6. **Primary metric:** harm-world power, with benign false alarm as a constraint. 7. **Cost:** none (shared labels). 8. **Should fail if:** the gate closes during real harm because the cost increment is noisy, losing the power the Brier version has. 9. **Falsification experiment:** gate vs no gate at identical settings.

## BD — decision-boundary-weighted querying
1. **Parent:** `pprm_tight`, `pp_cost_tight`. 2. **Change:** query probability `q_s = clip(frac · w(p_s) / w̄, 0.02, 1)` with `w(p) = exp(−½((logit p − logit τ)/h)²) + floor`, `w̄` its mean on a nominal source stream; the increment uses the matching propensity. 3. **Mechanism targeted:** F5/F2, label-limited power. 4. **Predicted direction:** more power at the same expected label rate when harm concentrates near the decision boundary. 5. **Matched baseline:** uniform `q = frac`. 6. **Primary metric:** harm-world power at **matched actual label count** (labels used are recorded from the realized mask). 7. **Cost:** none. 8. **Should fail if:** realized spend differs from uniform, or the added propensity variance outweighs the concentration. 9. **Falsification experiment:** `(h, floor) ∈ {(0.5, 0.25), (1.0, 0.25), (1.0, 0.5)}` vs uniform, compared at equal realized labels.

## Configurations (delay 5, tol 0, frac ∈ {0.10, 0.20})
DK 16 (2 parents × γ ∈ {1, 0.995, 0.99, 0.98} × 2 frac); HG 8 (`pprm_tight` × γ ∈ {1, 0.99} × w ∈ {100, 200} × 2 frac); BD 24 (2 parents × γ ∈ {1, 0.99} × 3 shapes × 2 frac). 48 configurations × 19 development worlds, 200 seeds, registry batch `q1_fixes`.

## Decision rules (vs the matched parent at identical frac/delay/tol, paired over the 9 harm worlds)
**Improvement:** mean power gain ≥ +0.05, improved in ≥ 7 of 9 harm worlds, no world worse by more than 0.05, null false alarm ≤ 0.07, and mean benign false alarm not higher by more than 0.02. Label-matching for BD: realized labels within ±10% of the parent.
**Finalist:** the best development configuration per fix that meets the rule is frozen (parameters recorded) and evaluated **once** on the held-out pool (`heldout_pool()` in `harness.py`, 400 seeds, seed block 5000) against the same parent and against Page–Hinkley. No re-tuning after seeing held-out results. G2 and G3 generator families, when implemented, form a second held-out confirmation and may not be used for selection.
**Beyond-parent claim:** a fix "beats the strongest valid baseline" only if it also meets the improvement rule against `pprm_tight` (not merely against its own parent).
