### P6 validity-scope correction — 2026-11-04

The focused audit's earlier claim that the terminal test count may depend on later query
availability is superseded by the inspected `run_p6` control flow. Whenever P6 tests, it uses the
fixed `n=min(B,50)` queried disagreement signs; if that count is not reached, the code does not
promote. Under P3b's eventwise fixed-delay conditional null, predictable query requests and
outcome-independent query randomization preserve a conditional success probability at most 1/2
for each queried sign in release order. Sequential stochastic domination then validates the
one-shot exact binomial upper-tail test at alpha .05. The protocol's weaker exchangeability wording
alone does not imply this guarantee (an exchangeable all-equal fair latent sign has all-success
probability 1/2). See the revised [P6 scope audit](b3_p6_validity_scope_2026-11-04.md).

This resolves the stopping/selection proof under the stated P3b null, but not the archived B3
runner-to-row identity, stochastic validity of deterministic real streams, or any SOTA claim.
Continue treating historical B3 outcomes as descriptive until the provenance gap is resolved.
