# 03. Paired inference protocol
Re-run of the frozen arms (`pprm_tight`, HG = `fix_brier` γ=.99 gate 100, delay 5, tol 0) on the G1/G2 held-out pools with the **original seed blocks** (5000/5500, 7000/7500), 400 seeds per world, saving per-seed alarm time, detection, false alarm, harm-before-alarm and labels (`outputs/hg_validation/03_PER_SEED_RESULTS.parquet`). `HG_unifcap` (uniform support cap, doc 02) is added as a **development refinement, not a frozen finalist**. Reproduction gate: reproduced per-world detection probabilities must equal the original summaries exactly — **passed (max abs difference 0.0 for G1 and G2)**.
Statistics: (i) *within-world* CI: seed bootstrap of the harm-world-averaged paired gain, worlds fixed; (ii) *across-world* CI: bootstrap over harm worlds of the per-world gains (3 worlds in G1, 4 in G2, so this interval is the honest generalisation uncertainty); (iii) exact sign test on discordant seed pairs, pooled over harm worlds (descriptive only, seeds within a world are not independent regimes). Practical threshold: the frozen +0.05 mean gain. No multiplicity correction is needed for the 4 frozen HG contrasts reported as such (each is a separate pre-specified cell); the unifcap rows are exploratory.
