\section{Real-data results: EIA reserve procurement}\label{sec:eia}

\paragraph{Task.}
Two balancing authorities (CISO and ERCO) publish hourly demand, day-ahead forecast, net generation and total interchange.
An hour is an event, the features are those four series, and the outcome is next-hour demand. A policy procures a reserve when its demand forecast exceeds
$\tau$, the $90$th percentile of next-hour demand over 2019--2024. A shortfall (no reserve, demand above $\tau$) costs $5$, an idle reserve costs $1$.
The deployed policy is a ridge regression frozen on 2019--2024, and the reference is a weekly-refit ridge with a $24$-hour label delay for its refits.
Reserve procurement does not change demand, so one realised outcome scores both policies, and the paired cost is exactly zero when the two actions agree.
Four registered paths cover CISO and ERCO in 2025 and 2026. Disagreement is rare on the real pair ($0.01$--$0.2\%$ of hours, and $2.2\%$ for CISO in 2026, which has $150$ disagreements),
so CISO-2026 is the informative null path.
Every parameter of the monitors, including the pacing seed and the injected-harm band, comes from a rehearsal one year earlier (fit on 2019--2023, run on 2024).
The protocol was frozen by hash before any monitor touched a real path.

\paragraph{Validity.}
The registered null swaps the roles of the two policies on each event with an independent fair coin, so the monitor sees $S_tZ_t$ with $S_t\in\{-1,+1\}$.
Conditional on the real path, the null holds exactly, and the check runs per path under real covariates, outcomes and temporal dependence.
We ran $1{,}000$ replicates per path with shared coins across arms: four paths, each with one full-feedback cell and four arms at four budgets, $68$ cells at $\varepsilon=0.1$.
All $68$ passed: no arm alarmed in any replicate (largest exact one-sided $95\%$ upper bound $0.30\%$).
At the sharper boundary $\varepsilon=0$ the largest alarm rate was $28/1{,}000$ (upper bound $3.82\%$). On CISO-2026 the fixed gated arms alarmed in $2.7\%$ of replicates,
the paced gated arms in $1.6$--$2.8\%$, the empirical-Bernstein arm in $0.3$--$0.8\%$, and ungated HT in $0$--$0.7\%$.
On the unmodified streams the deterministic full-feedback monitor did not alarm on any path, and no selective-label arm did either, apart from ungated HT at $r=0.20$ on CISO-2026 (2 of 200 replicates).

\paragraph{Injected harm.}
To create a positive control with a known onset, we inject under-procurement into the real paths. After the onset (at $25\%$, $50\%$ or $75\%$ of the path), every event where the deployed policy procures
with a forecast margin above $\tau$ in $(0,m]$ is switched to no reserve. The band limit $m$ is the median positive margin in the 2024 rehearsal ($3{,}875$ MW for CISO, $6{,}424$ MW for ERCO).
The harm is seasonal, since it bites only when forecast demand is near $\tau$, so detection delays run to hundreds or thousands of hours.
Of the twelve path-by-onset cells, ten contain detectable injected harm and full feedback detects all ten.
(CISO-2025 at the $75\%$ onset has no affected events, and in ERCO-2025 at $75\%$ even full feedback does not detect the $122$ affected events within the window.)
Table~\ref{tab:eia} reports detection over $200$ query replicates per cell, and no arm alarmed before onset in any replicate.

From a $5\%$ budget upward, fixed gating matches the full-feedback median delay in all ten cells (for example $1{,}232$ hours for both on CISO-2026 with a $50\%$ onset), using $338$ to $701$ labels.
At $r=0.05$ ungated HT is slower in that cell ($1{,}884$ hours), and at $r=0.20$ on ERCO-2026 with a $75\%$ onset it detects $6\%$ of replicates while every gated HT arm detects all of them.
At $r=0.02$ the reserve of about $140$--$170$ labels runs out before the summer harm, which explains the low fixed-gating detection in that column.

\paragraph{Pacing replicates on real paths.}
After onset, injected disagreements rise from about $0.1\%$ to $6$--$15\%$ of events. The past-only $200$-event pacer then pushes $q$ toward its $0.10$ floor, and the bound $5/q+\varepsilon$ grows with it.
Paced gating is slower than fixed gating on these paths and the empirical-Bernstein engine is slowest, the same ordering as the synthetic intervention sweep of Section~\ref{sec:pacing}.
A pacing floor and window are therefore design choices to register in any prospective deployment.
