**Classification: F. NO_GO** for the primary claim (a novel dependence-robust, anytime-valid decision-harm monitor).
Why F and not another class: (a) the target guarantee for the economically natural stationary-average null is impossible without structure (Theorem B1) and has no useful power for any test, even a perfectly informed oracle, in the persistence range of interest (oracle power 0.07-0.48); (b) the null that *is* testable at the monitors' own level (H0-cond) is already served by known martingale methodology and by the repaired monitors; the observed "dependence failure" is a null-class mismatch, not a bug; (c) the fully observed finite-state version is published (Sethi et al., ICML 2026). The decision does **not** depend on the unresolved part of Gate C, because Gate B forecloses a non-vacuous contribution regardless of novelty. Not G, since the decisive question was answerable; not C, because the only proved result (B1) is elementary and probably folklore, with no empirical operating regime attached.
Secondary results (report separately, not as the main claim): repaired HG improves a local PPRM-style parent (+0.029 / +0.052, Holm-significant) — an **E-type composition/engineering result** with no superiority over PH / CUSUM; the covariate-dependent-cap audit finding (a genuine, repaired correctness defect); the elementary persistence obstruction with the oracle power table (a useful *negative result* for the paper's motivation).
