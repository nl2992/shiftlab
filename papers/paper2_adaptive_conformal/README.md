# Paper 2 support: conformal baselines

This directory records fixed and adaptive conformal prediction baselines. Under the current
[research charter](../../docs/research_charter.md), conformal prediction is a supporting uncertainty
module, not by itself the Paper 2 contribution. The candidate Paper 2 question is statistically
certifying a frozen candidate's net decision benefit before promotion, with fresh evaluation and
valid handling of candidate selection, multiplicity, costs, and rollback. The current conformal
results do not establish that contribution or a decision-safety guarantee.
