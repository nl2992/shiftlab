# Research log

2026-10-19: Implemented first shared CPU baseline suite. No new method proposed. The initial three-seed grid is exploratory; failures of a baseline or a proposed hypothesis require fresh replicated confirmation.

2026-10-24: Expanded the delayed-feedback benchmark with bounded-mean full-stream betting and a prespecified eight-start convex mixture, then calibrated both alongside the Hoeffding process on 2,000 independent stationary and benign-variance streams per query-rate cell. All exact two-sided 95% null upper limits were at most 2.14%. The preregistered late-onset, weak-harm alternative detected 933/2,000 streams with the restart mixture versus 100/2,000 with full-stream betting; a second disjoint 2,000-seed block reproduced 895/2,000 versus 100/2,000. A 12-cell factorial with 2,000 paired seeds per cell found positive restart-mixture detection gains in every cell (1.2–68.4 percentage points), with all exact paired tests significant after Holm adjustment. This establishes a strong simulator-specific baseline result, not a novel method or real-data guarantee; delayed-label informativeness, serial dependence, policy adaptation, and causal decision utility remain untested. The protocols, row-level metrics and hashes are in docs/delayed_null_calibration_protocol.md and outputs/delayed_null_calibration_2000, outputs/delayed_feedback_confirmation_2000, outputs/delayed_feedback_replication_2000, and outputs/delayed_feedback_grid_confirmation_2000.

2026-10-24: Extended the history-predictable query plus eventwise PPI-bound monitor to deterministic 12-event delayed feedback under the two-state Markov-volatility martingale null. The fresh 2,000-stream-per-cell null calibration had predictable-bound alarm rates 1.40% and 1.25%; all four Bonferroni simultaneous exact upper limits were at most 2.12%. A disjoint confirmation of 2,000 paired streams per harm dose passed the frozen retention rule: macro detection gain +6.025 pp (95% paired-bootstrap CI [+5.325,+6.750]) and label difference −130.264 per stream (CI [−134.224,−126.377]). Dose gains were +0.65, +1.90, +6.30, and +15.25 pp for effects 0.04, 0.20, 0.40, and 0.60. The predictable system produced 31/2,000 pre-onset alarms per dose block; these count as nondetections. This supports the standard bounded-factor monitor-query composition only under the registered statewise martingale null, frozen policy pair/proxy, and complete independent delay-12 feedback. Query paths diverge, so the bound alone is not identified as the cause; there is no novelty claim. See docs/delayed_markov_bound_confirmation_results.md, the frozen protocol, and outputs/delayed_markov_predictable_{development_60,calibration_2000,confirmation_2000}/.

2026-10-24: Ran the frozen matched-path support-bound ablation under the same delayed Markov process. With exogenous precommitted query schedules, fixed and eventwise systems had identical requested labels and evidence horizons, isolating the bound. Constant q=0.20 gave exactly identical outcomes. Across periodic q schedules {0.05,0.35}, {0.05,0.55}, and {0.05,0.80}, eventwise-minus-fixed macro detection gains were +7.08, +4.58, and +7.50 points (60 paired seeds; descriptive seed-bootstrap 95% intervals [+3.33,+11.25], [+1.25,+8.75], [+3.75,+11.67]). Mean realized labels were 473.75–475.80 with a 480 cap. Each null cell had 0–2 alarms, not calibration. This supports the eventwise range mechanism only in the registered development DGP and shows the q schedule itself remains to be validated; no new betting method or theorem is claimed. Protocol, result, script and stream metrics: docs/matched_path_bound_ablation_protocol.md, docs/matched_path_bound_ablation_results.md, scripts/run_matched_path_bound_ablation.py, outputs/matched_path_bound_ablation_60/.

2026-10-24: The frozen `q∈{0.05,0.80}` matched-path candidate passed its independent 2,000-stream-per-cell calibration (largest simultaneous upper limit 3.29%) and disjoint confirmation (2,000 paired streams per dose). Eventwise-minus-fixed macro detection gain was +5.4125 points (95% paired-bootstrap CI [+4.5875,+6.2500]) with exactly identical labels and event prefixes; the +2-point detection and no-extra-label retention gates passed. Dose gains were -0.20, +0.55, +4.85, +16.45 points, concentrating on stronger harm. Eventwise null rates were 2.40% and 2.10%, above fixed-bound 0.75% and 0.50% but within the registered 5% simultaneous gate. The result isolates a tighter predictable support range on common query paths for this one synthetic schedule; it does not establish a new e-process or general superiority. See docs/matched_path_bound_confirmation_results.md, docs/matched_path_bound_confirmation_protocol.md, and outputs/matched_path_bound_{calibration_2000,confirmation_2000}/.

2026-10-24: Screened predictable convex reweighting of the existing five bounded stakes on matched eventwise-bound, periodic-query, delay-12 paths. The 60-seed development grid nominated eta=0 (uniform per-event rebalancing) over the eta=1 fixed mixture: 27/240 versus 15/240 detections, paired macro difference +5.00 pp (95% seed-bootstrap CI [+2.50,+7.92]); all four dose point estimates favored the candidate. This winner is not history-adaptive because eta=0 fixes weights uniformly at each event. On the frozen independent 2,000-stream-per-cell calibration, candidate exact-null alarms were 88/2,000 (4.40%; Bonferroni simultaneous upper 5.54%), so it failed the preregistered <=5% upper-limit gate. The fixed mixture had 48/2,000 (2.40%; upper 3.29%); both methods passed the strict-negative control cell and had no support/budget violations. The disjoint harm confirmation was correctly not run. This is a failed advancement, not evidence that the candidate's true type-I rate exceeds 5%, and its development power is not confirmatory. The predictable convex-factor argument is standard and no novelty is claimed.
