# Prior-art boundary for the certified adaptive fixed-cohort separation

**Date:** 2026-10-28. **Status:** focused source-level boundary; G1 remains unresolved.

## What the new exact separation says

`switching_fixed_cohort_vs_adaptive_protocol_v1.md` certifies a strict gap between a full causal adaptive oracle and the best randomized fixed-cohort terminal test in one registered `T=6, B=3, d=1` recurrent-HMM cell. This proves the fixed-cohort reference is too restrictive to explain the oracle value. It does not establish the source of the gap, an algorithm, a growing-family theorem, or an advantage over adaptive sequential design.

## Closest audited ingredients

- **Delayed economic sampling/adoption:** Chick, Forster and Pertile study sequential experimentation with a finite sample cap, per-query sampling cost, response delay and adoption cost; the repository's [source audit](switching_delayed_comparator_audit_v1.md) inspected their Bellman equations and proposition. This is close to a one-promotion restriction, while recurrent rollback and the causal-null risk contract are absent.
- **Controlled sensing with error and sensing-switch cost:** Prabhu et al.'s audited sequential multi-hypothesis test adaptively selects sensing arms and stops under a uniform terminal error constraint with sensing-switch costs; see [the repository audit](switching_sequential_safety_prior_v1.md). The source has stationary parametric observations, no delayed queue/pathwise query cap, and switches sensing rather than deployment.
- **Finite-horizon utility-driven sensing:** Ferrari, Zhao and Scaglione jointly optimize sensing-resource choice, stopping, and exploitation in a belief-state POMDP; see [the repository audit](switching_finite_horizon_sensing_prior_audit_v1.md). Their source lacks delayed labels, pathwise cumulative query rights, robust causal-null risk, and repeated deployment reversal.
- **Chance-constrained POMDP planning:** RAO* computes finite-horizon policies for expected performance under a probabilistic safety constraint. The primary MIT record describes its execution-risk pruning and optimal deterministic policies for the modeled CC-POMDP ([record](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/839ffa98-84e4-4d87-8b3d-1499a1f5cdcb)). Its single stochastic-model chance constraint does not by itself encode a conditional-null adversary that sees pending outcomes hidden from the learner.
- **Robust finite-memory POMDPs:** the 2025 RFPG source optimizes finite-state controllers for worst-case reachability across a finite set of hidden POMDP models ([IJCAI source](https://ijcai.org/proceedings/2025/947)). The paper itself describes local subgradient optimization without a global optimality guarantee. Its objective is worst-case model performance, rather than expected economic utility under one alternative plus a separate worst-case conditional-null risk constraint.
- **Budgeted observation-capability selection:** Konsta, Lluch Lafuente and Matheja formulate a fixed-budget observability problem for POMDPs and give algorithms for a positional-strategy fragment ([CAV record](https://orbit.dtu.dk/en/publications/what-should-be-observed-foroptimal-reward-inpomdps/)). The public record was inspected, but the publisher PDF fetch was denied; use this only as a pointer that budgeted observation design is established, not as a theorem-equivalence claim.

## Reduction assessment

The ingredients overlap strongly enough to reject claims that adaptive query choice, utility-aware stopping, hidden-Markov reversal, a finite-horizon risk constraint, or finite-memory robust control are individually new. No audited source in this focused set proves the exact combined contract: one policy faces an expected-cost HMM alternative and a separate history-adaptive conditional-null risk process; labels are paired, delayed and pathwise-budgeted; deployment switches repeatedly and pays costs. The finite sequence-form LP is a generic extensive-form solution for tiny horizons, not a scalable contribution. A source gap is therefore still a hypothesis, not a novelty conclusion.

For the registered cell, the information structure can be reduced exactly to a finite two-player zero-sum extensive-form game. The learner's realization plan minimizes expected cost under the HMM chance law. A second player selects each queried label's conditional success probability in `[0,1/2]` after seeing the full event history, including queried labels still pending for the learner. The second player's terminal payoff is one if any promotion occurred. The learner's information sets omit unreleased labels, so this is not the ordinary common-belief POMDP used by the cited chance-constrained and semi-Markov methods. Because the null reachability probability is multilinear in the conditional probabilities, a maximizing response exists at endpoint probabilities `{0,1/2}`. The sequence-form LP enforces risk against every such response while action feasibility enforces the pathwise budget. This is a faithful exact reduction, but sequence-form optimization is generic and its full-history size grows exponentially; it is not the missing scalable algorithm.

The next G1 test must be stronger than another restricted-class comparison: either (a) construct a mathematically faithful composition of controlled sensing, delayed economic stopping and fresh-risk accounting on the same contract and show it matches the exact oracle over a nontrivial family, or (b) prove a growing-family separation from that composition while preserving its information, risk and budget resources. Until one occurs, do not select a method or start efficacy/real-data evaluation.

## Claim separation

The causal-null bound concerns false promotion. The HMM belief concerns expected value under the working alternative. Neither an e-process accumulated before a reversal nor the global false-promotion guarantee establishes current-regime benefit or future profitability after reversal. Any composition baseline must carry these quantities separately.
