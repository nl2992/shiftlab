# HG e15: matched information, parent gain, and dependence limit (partial W01/W03/W04/W08)

`audit_hg_e15.py` pinned the archived `perseed.parquet`, `comparisons.csv`, and `arm_summary.csv` by SHA-256 and recomputed all 30 archived paired comparison point estimates from 168,000 seed-arm rows across 15 harm and six null worlds. The maximum point-estimate discrepancy is below 1e−16. At each nominal 10% and 20% label fraction, HG, its PPRM parent, Page–Hinkley, and CUSUM have **identical pathwise label counts** on matched world/seed keys. The last two use independently calibrated fixed-horizon thresholds and are different-guarantee comparators despite equal labels.

HG minus PPRM power is +0.028833 at 10% and +0.051833 at 20%, with HG cumulative harm before alarm 15.20% and 25.14% lower in harm worlds. The archived cluster-bootstrap intervals and Holm p-values are +0.0288 [0.0122, 0.0463], p=.004, and +0.0518 [0.0198, 0.0903], p=.0025. A fresh replay of the archived analyzer on the stored e15 seed rows reproduced all 30 comparison rows, including the 4,000-resample intervals and Holm values, exactly; it is an analysis replay, not an independent bootstrap-method check or fresh simulator run. The older “~21/24 harm reduction” phrase referred to *raw cost units averaged over harm and null worlds* and must not be used as a harm-world percentage. Against Page–Hinkley, HG power is −0.065667 and −0.110167 at the same label counts, so a positive HG advantage over PH is unsupported. The source's two-sided Holm values (1.0 and .3225) are not an equivalence test or proof of PH superiority.

The 20% HG false-alarm rate in the e15 GARCH finance null is 137/1000 (Wilson interval in the archived report [.117, .160]); the clustered null is 79/1000. Both exceed nominal .05, and these dependent-null settings are outside HG's stated conditional-null guarantee. The broader “up to .23” figure comes from the separate e14 development panel: `audit_hg_dependence_e14.py` verifies 93/400 = .2325 false alarms for the HG parent on `D2|null|fin_garch_a.10b.88`. Its long-run-variance F2 fix reduces the worst null rate to .0475, but the stored four-world iid-harm power change is −.0500893, just beyond the registered −.05 loss limit; this is a near-miss of a development rule, not a prospective validity repair. The finite-support cap unit test was freshly run and passed, but that test cannot extend the guarantee to dependent outcomes or prove whole-algorithm filtration.

This is analysis-level reproduction of stored seed outcomes, not a fresh generator/simulator run. It supports a narrow within-parent power/harm result and a documented dependence failure, while leaving literature novelty, real-data value, and universal comparator dominance unsupported.
