# Three historical gate roles and an Atlas chronology correction

This is a partial W02 audit. `04_GATE_AND_THRESHOLD_AUDIT.csv` records the individual rules and their sources. The old thresholds remain the historical decision rules; the observations below do not reopen their original passes or failures.

**WP2 G1 (analytic design screen).** The frozen protocol `docs/stratified_harm_monitor_development_protocol.md` recorded a best-K growth ratio of at least 1.5 against the strongest applicable incumbent, plus a homogeneous-growth retention rule of at least 0.8 against gated growth. The incumbent included an equally equipped known subgroup composition, expected to be algebraically identical. `scripts/retrospective_audit/recompute_wp2.py` hashed all five archived outputs against the run manifest, reconciled 19,440 raw learner-slice rows against 19,440 scalar rows and 972 mean-summary groups, and recomputed all 36 G1 decisions with zero aggregate mismatches (`WP2_RAW_RECOMPUTATION.json`). Across 2,160 matched candidate/composition rows, the maximum growth difference was 1.04e-17; the stored per-row identity diagnostic reaches 4.44e-16. The eligible candidate/strongest ratios range 0.4030–1.0000, so the 1.5 screen fails. Relaxing it cannot show an advantage over an equal-information identical construction. Comparisons to pooled harm have a different null. This is an archived-arithmetic replication; learner slices and outcome/query expectations were not independently regenerated from the generator.

**WP3 (20-seed development screen).** The frozen `docs/data_efficient_edetector_prescreen_protocol.md` required a primary +10 detection percentage points or 1.5 ratio in censored delay against *every* applicable comparator, at no greater average labels and dollars, plus seven secondary-cell rules. Independent row-level reconciliation in `WP3_PATH_RECOMPUTATION.json` and `WP3_PAIRED_COMPARATOR_AUDIT.json` reproduces all 1,960 arm/cell aggregates and all 1,696 eligible paired comparisons. At the primary cell, the candidate detects 11/20 versus the fastest of five 20/20 comparators, and fails 48/208 pairwise gates. Its 48.05 labels and $71.50 are below that comparator's 110.40 and $165.59, so the resource saving is real within the archived paths, but the *all-comparator* advancement rule fails. The best observed comparator and its conditional interval are selected on these exposed paths; this was never an independent confirmation.
