## Findings
1. **Null-class mismatch (central).** The twin null is null only over the training-split and role coins
   (`docs/sota_sprint/p2_known_null_protocol.md` step 4: "For any single split one twin may be slightly better"). Each certificate's
   guarantee is conditional on the fixed models, so a promotion in a split where the challenger is genuinely better is not a false
   promotion under that guarantee. ✓ Per-split advantage (iid z, which overstates significance under positive autocorrelation):
   appliances 16/20 splits |z|>2 (11 favour the challenger), 45.8 % of episodes z>1.645; beijing 12/20, bike 10/20, household 6/20,
   metro 8/20; split-level SD vs |mean| advantage 0.0281/0.0077 (appliances).
2. **Attribution confounded.** "Under serial dependence" is not identified: a per-split fixed effect alone pushes marginal promotion
   above α even without dependence. → tested by the registered controls (`P2_CONTROLS_REGISTRATION.md`).
3. **Replicate unit.** All 20 splits share each dataset's evaluation rows; split-clustered CIs ignore evaluation-stream uncertainty;
   the effective unit is 5 datasets.
4. **Comparator labels.** `xu_acquisition_only` is a ShiftLab certificate + Xu acquisition composite and should not be listed as a
   powered published certified method; all comparators are common-contract adapters, none author-native.
5. **Exposure.** Protocol 36d97966 precedes build d418f5c3 and results 8305d4f2 (ordering fine), but the five streams were already used
   for cost runs and persistence estimates ⇒ exposed, not confirmation.
6. **Superseded code.** Fixes `c66185c0` (adaptive-range propensity ordering) and `81dc4d34` (MOPE interval intersection) live only on
   `origin/codex/sprint2-leaderboards`; they affect the gated ports (4–5 of the K1 ports). "K1 PASS 9/9" is not current until replayed.
   SMCS, Choe–Ramdas and Xu are unaffected.
7. **Multiplicity.** K1 (≥5/9 ports on any of 4 datasets) is a low bar, but the largest cells would survive adjustment.
8. **Prior art.** Conditional vs unconditional predictive-ability distinction (Giacomini & White 2006, already conceded in
   `docs/sprint2/wp2/protocol.md`). "Anytime-valid tests fail a null they were not built for" is not novel; measured deployment
   economics could be. Literature not searched online by the reviewer.

## Reviewer's strongest defensible claim
On five exposed UCI streams with exchangeable-twin models (null only over the training-split randomisation), conditional-null
certificate adapters promote often (up to .428 SMCS) but mostly promote the realised-better twin at little all-in cost, while
constant-policy Model Picker/CAMS adapters promote on .72–.93 of runs at up to +25.7 cost — so "marginal false-promotion rate" and
"conditional validity / deployment cost" rank methods differently.

Reviewer rating: **D, with a path to C**; not B until a dependence-specific effect is shown and the code-fix replay is done.
Single most decisive test: the time-permutation and coin-swap controls (now registered and running).
