## 1. Claimed contributions (as read)
C1. Pathwise (fixed-sequence, any nonanticipating delay rule) (R,delta) budget on realised harm H_t = k*N_t + excess loss vs keeping incumbent, enforced by a shield with rollback reserve k, pending-feedback reserve (worst-case 1/pi), and Bennett/Ville width for design-based sampling (Thm 1).
C2. Lower bounds: no payback certificate from delayed feedback (Prop 1, essentially an indistinguishability argument) and necessity of the rollback/exposure reserve for a first promotion, R >= min(2k+e, k+n) (Prop 2); shield attains it on the all-harm tape.
C3. Empirical: registered simulation, 12 real streams; regime map (exposure per delay window vs headroom h = R-2k); comparators exceed budget.

## 3. Contributions already covered by prior work?
No single named paper contains C1 or C2. Findings by component:
- The mechanism "play the risky arm only if a worst-case check on accumulated outcome passes, else fall back to baseline" IS the conservative-bandit template (Wu 2016; Garcelon 2020). The paper admits it ("same family"). Shield + reserve is the same idea with pessimistic accounting. Reviewer will say the algorithmic idea is incremental; the delta is the accounting for (a) fees with pre-paid rollback, (b) in-transit labels at worst case, (c) IPW-sampled labels with Ville width.
- Prop 1 (no payback certificate without restricting the future) is a standard indistinguishability argument; covered in spirit by the generic "future cannot be certified from the past" arguments in safe-bandit/OPE literature. Treat as a clarifying proposition, not a theorem-level contribution.
- Prop 2 reserve necessity: proof is a 3-line indistinguishability argument (copy environment before j, then all-harm). Prudent-Banker claims matching lower bounds in a different (regret) metric, so "lower bounds exist for delayed baseline safety" is not new in kind; this exact pathwise reserve bound (R >= min(2k+e,k+n)) is not in anything I found. Mathematically light, and it is a bound on the worst-case harm achievable by any valid controller, not on cost of safety.
- Key structural assumption doing heavy lifting: full-information via shadow mode (a label reveals both losses; g_t known before acting; agreement rounds are harm-free). This removes the bandit-feedback difficulty that conservative-bandit literature deals with. Not a coverage issue, but a reviewer will see the setting as an expert/full-info problem with delays; position against delayed full-information online learning and against sequential-testing-with-delays more than bandits.
- Anytime-valid IPW/Bennett width under adaptive label queries: Xu et al. 2024 (uncited) and betting CS (cited) cover the machinery; the paper itself says "Confidence sequences of this type are standard", fairly honest.

## 5. Verdicts
- C1 pathwise harm budget with shield: NOVEL IN COMBINATION / INCREMENTAL IN IDEA. Template is conservative bandits; I found no paper that gives a hard pathwise budget including switching fees under arbitrary delays plus design-based sampling. The theorem proof is short (deterministic accounting + Bennett + Ville) so the contribution is the formulation and accounting, not technique. Verdict under limits: PROCEED (delta statable in one sentence: additive hard cap on fee-inclusive excess loss vs incumbent under arbitrary delays and sampled labels).
- C2 lower bounds: INCREMENTAL. Correct and tidy but trivial indistinguishability; the "matching" claim is limited to the first promotion and a feedback-blind window. Do not oversell as a general lower bound on the cost of safety.
- C3 regime map / empirics: NOVEL AS A FINDING, but the paper itself labels the retained-gain association empirical; novelty depends on the registered design, not on theory. Real-data part is simulated delay/dropout.
- Overall provisional score 5-6/10: clear neighbours (Wu 2016, Prudent-Banker 2026, Drift2Act) with a defensible delta. No named paper contains the result, so not ABANDON-level. Caveat: same-family reviewer, abstract-level checks only.
