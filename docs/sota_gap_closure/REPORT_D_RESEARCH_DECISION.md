# Report D - research decision
Synthetic evidence only; no real data touched. No VERIFIED_REPRODUCTION of any published baseline exists (sources unreachable), so "SOTA" means source-inspired local baselines.
1. **Principal candidate: HG** (decision-cost-gated discounted prediction-risk betting) in its repaired form. Confirmed on new G3-G6 pools against its parent PPRM-style baseline: +0.029 [+0.012, +0.046] (Holm p 0.004) at 10% labels, +0.052 [+0.020, +0.090] (Holm p 0.003) at 20%, with -20.608 [-29.694, -11.802] / -24.215 [-33.546, -15.217] harm accrued before the alarm.
2. **Is HG still competitive?** Against Page-Hinkley and CUSUM it is *not dominant*: HG vs PH power -0.066 [-0.240, +0.079] (Holm p 1.000) (10%) and -0.110 [-0.247, +0.005] (Holm p 0.323) (20%), both n.s. after Holm with point estimates favouring PH, while HG alarms earlier when it alarms (harm before alarm -23.365 [-42.801, -5.022] at 20%) and carries an anytime guarantee under its null (PH / CUSUM thresholds are empirical). In the cost-aware environment (Q3) HG-triggered promotion has higher regret than PH-triggered promotion under persistent harm. HG is **invalid under dependent outcomes** (measured false-alarm rates up to 0.23, shared by pprm and the cost process).
3. **Is another candidate better?** For certification, CAMS-style disagreement querying (no S09 extension improves on it). For monitoring, no tested variant is better than HG after correction for multiple comparisons: the robust mixture (+0.018 [+0.001, +0.033] (Holm p 0.267) at 10%) and every corrected-floor rule (round 2) failed confirmation; the LRV bet loses power (-0.061 [-0.079, -0.044] (Holm p 0.003)).
4. **Does any improvement survive a properly matched SOTA comparison?** Only HG > pprm (the PPRM-inspired parent). Improvements over Page-Hinkley / CUSUM were not found.
5. **Defensible novel mechanism?** Not established; literature novelty unassessed. Defensible findings: the covariate-dependent bet cap voids the supermartingale step (exact counterexample, modest empirical size effect, clean repair); the predictable-bound tax on non-uniform querying and its dependence on the propensity floor; the disagreement-event cap in certification.
6. **Real-data validation?** Justified only as a stress test of the dependence failure (financial streams are exactly the clustered / volatility-clustered case), not as a performance test of HG: the earlier CRSP replay already showed DK worse than its parent, and synthetic size inflation under dependence is the main unresolved risk.
**Decision: continue with a pivot.** Keep HG as the repaired baseline; make a dependence-robust (conditional-null-safe) e-process the primary target (ranked first in `NOVELTY_OPPORTUNITY_RANKING.md`); do not claim a detection advantage over classical detectors or on real data before that is solved.
