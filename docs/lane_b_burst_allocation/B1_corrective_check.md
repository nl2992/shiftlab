# B1 corrective check (registered in LANE_CONTROL tick 3; spec not changed after results)

**Status: Monte Carlo development evidence, one registered check, not confirmatory.**
Code: `scripts/lane_b/separation_check.py corrective 1000 1000` (about 2 min). Tests:
`tests/test_lane_b_separation.py` (29 pass, ruff clean). Grid: `B1_corrective_check_grid.csv`.
The first check is still reproducible (`monitor="single"` is the default).

## Setup

- H: Z mean 0.5 on the burst [500,800) only, mean -0.2 after (null holds). N unchanged (-0.2 in the burst, 0.5 from 800).
  Same D in H and N, D=0 before 500, rho in {0.05, 0.2}, variant-B event scoring (e-process updates on
  disagreements only; q=0 disagreements are predictable skips), Z=+-1, eps=0.1, C=1.
- Monitor: all-onset mixture, one default-stake `PredictableBoundMeanEProcess` component per start in
  0,25,...,1975 (80 starts, weights 1/80, components at one before their start; checked against
  `PredictableStartedMixtureEProcess` to 1e-8). Alarm when the mixture is at least 1/alpha, recorded at t+d.
- Every rule from the first check (oblivious a/b/c, r1 SPRT-stop, r2 DE-CuSum-style h in {3,10,30,100}), B in {40,60,80,120}, alpha in
  {0.05, 0.001}, d in {0, 20}, 1000 H/N seed pairs and 1000 null seeds per cell. Null = mean eps everywhere (boundary).
- **Seeds.** Cell j=(rho_idx*2+d_idx)*4+B_idx, base = 100600000 + (j%8)*10000 + 1000 + (j//8)*2000; H/N rows base+[0,1000),
  null rows base+1000+[0,1000). These sit in the unused gaps [1000,5000) of strides 0-7 and are disjoint from all first-check
  seeds (a test enforces this). **Correction to the first check:** its map `100600000+k*10000`, k up to 23, spilled past the
  registered block end 100699999 for k>=10 (variants A and B2 and parts of B). Those results stand but their seeds are partly
  outside the registry block; the lead should record that.
- No headline family was pre-registered, so the criterion is evaluated separately for each of the 8 (rho, d, alpha) families.
  "Best oblivious" and "best responsive" maximise min(P_H,P_N) over their own grids on the same seeds (18 oblivious configs
  against 1 + 4 responsive), so the selection is optimistic for the oblivious side. This makes the gap conservative against the separation.

## Verdict (pre-registered criterion: separation FAILS if at every tight B in {40,60,80} some oblivious rule is within 0.05 of or above the best responsive rule)

- **The criterion is met (separation fails) in 2 of 8 families**, both at alpha=0.001, d=20 (rho 0.05 and 0.2). There the best min P
  is at most 0.08 for every rule, so nothing detects.
- **It is not met (separation NOT refuted) in 6 of 8 families**, in all four d=0 families and the two alpha=0.05, d=20 families.
  The criterion does not allow a single aggregate verdict beyond this split.
- d=0, alpha=0.05: the best responsive rule (r1 except r2 at rho=0.2, B=40) beats the best oblivious rule (always paced or a burst cap with a small b) by +0.11/+0.29/+0.32
  at B=40/60/80 (rho 0.05) and +0.09/+0.21/+0.36 (rho 0.2). The oblivious rules cannot do both: with a transient burst, "skip the burst" misses H and "spend on the
  burst" wastes labels in N.
- Context at d=0, alpha=0.05, rho=0.05: B=80 r1 has P_H 0.656, P_N 0.608 (delay avg 736, about 56 labels) while best oblivious (paced) has
  0.289/0.327 (delay 1094). At B=120 it is 0.775/0.628 against 0.549/0.542.
- At d=20 the picture is mixed. Responsive rules lose to oblivious at B=40 and 60 (e.g. alpha=0.05, rho=0.2: -0.060 and -0.066), because pending
  labels are charged at request and the SPRT stop arrives about 20 labels late; only at B=80 does r1 lead (+0.17 and +0.07).
- At alpha=0.001 the gaps are small in absolute terms (max +0.12 at d=0) and min P stays below 0.17 for every tight B, which is an unpowered regime.
  The conjectured growth of the gap as alpha falls is not seen. The largest gap in each family is smaller at alpha=0.001 than at 0.05.
- r2 (DE-CuSum) is the best responsive rule in only 4 of 24 tight-B comparisons (mostly B=40, h=30/100); r1 dominates, so the evidence-responsive win is the SPRT-stop rule, not the on-off one.

## Null control

Mean = eps everywhere, every rule, cell and alpha (368 runs per alpha, 1000 seeds each): worst observed false-alarm rate 0.018 at alpha=0.05
(largest exact one-sided 95% upper bound 0.0266, below 0.05) and 1/1000 at alpha=0.001 (mean rate 3.5e-05; the exact
bound cannot fall below 0.003 at n=1000, so alpha=0.001 validity is supported, not certified). Per-rule counts and exact bounds are in the tables and CSV.

## Selection caveats and limits

- Best-of-grid picks are made on the same seeds as the evaluation (no held-out half), and the families are not independent tests.
- P standard error up to 0.016 per entry; the 0.05 margin is about 3 standard errors of a difference.
- r1 uses a known harmful mean (0.5) in its SPRT, a mild oracle. The mixture grid (every 25 events) and the transient design are the lead's registered choices.
- Absolute detection is low at tight B, because the transient burst offers about 300 harmful disagreements but a hard cap of 40-80 labels, and
  the mixture pays for its all-onset prior. So "separation" here is a relative statement at low power, and says nothing about confirmatory significance.
- Even where it holds, the responsive winner is an SPRT stop plus the shared mixture; B0 flagged this as a combination of known pieces.
