# Integrated ShiftGuard efficacy: stored-seed check (partial W01/W03/W08)

`scripts/retrospective_audit/audit_integrated_efficacy.py` verified nine archived data/analysis hashes from the independent `analysis_v3/verification.json`, loaded the 7,200 stored seed-method metrics, and recomputed all 72 profile-method mean expected/realized utility and label-use values. Every profile-method cell has 100 unique seeds. All 22 predeclared primary paired expected-utility contrast means match `analysis_v3/primary_contrasts.csv` within 1e-9. No row exceeds its hard 640-label cap; the maximum observed use is 640. Detailed cell and contrast values are in `INTEGRATED_EFFICACY_SEED_AUDIT.json`.

The full pipeline's mean expected incremental utility versus continue is +118.006 for boundary-local harm and +161.161 when adaptation begins at the monitor, but −143.758 under post-promotion reversal and zero for the native late-onset profile. In the two aligned positive profiles, full minus two-gate is −68.530 and −40.095, and full minus fixed-candidate certification is −129.508 and −82.317. These are the archived seed-mean comparisons, not a claim that the simpler methods dominate universally. `audit_integrated_inference.py` independently recomputed all 22 registered one-sided paired t-tests, sequential 20,000-draw descriptive bootstrap intervals, Holm-adjusted p-values, and Bonferroni simultaneous lower bounds from stored seed vectors. Maximum absolute difference from the archived table was 1.43e−14. Four positive contrasts reject after Holm: full versus continue in the two aligned harm profiles, and full versus two-gate or fixed certification under reversal. Eleven contrasts are exact zero vectors with undefined t-test p-values; the archived analysis assigns them adjusted p=1, not evidence of equivalence. These are conditional calculations on the archived seeds; normality/coverage, protocol chronology, and selection remain distinct validity questions. Neither the 23.04-million-row learner log nor the equally sized evaluator log was replayed from the simulator; the independent historical verifier's `passed` receipt is provenance to inspect further, not a substitute for this audit's own event-level checks.

`audit_integrated_event_logs.py` also streamed all 7,200 paired learner/evaluator row groups (23.04 million events per log). Each stream has ordered event indices and stage boundaries; learner and evaluator actions, queried true/observed labels, per-event intervention costs, trigger/rollback events, and seed-level expected/realized utility reconcile. The logs contain 2,007,414 queried labels and expose no selected candidate ID before their recorded availability flag. Maximum seed utility arithmetic error is 2.28e−13. This is an event-log consistency check. The logs are generated retrospectively by `_build_event_logs` after `run_seed` has computed actions and outcomes, so schema separation and timestamps alone cannot prove the policy was adapted only to past information; source-code causal ordering and an independent simulator replay still need review.

The result supports a negative decision about *general superiority of this integrated configuration* alongside narrow positive aligned-profile effects. It does not establish that every possible composition fails, nor does it support a real-data integrated intervention claim. The 2026-10-26 method NO-GO remains a historical research-priority decision, not a statistical proof of no possible new algorithm.
