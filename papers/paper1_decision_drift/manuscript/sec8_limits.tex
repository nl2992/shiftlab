\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limits}

The synthetic streams use imposed costs and a single deployed-versus-reference pair per scenario, and the EIA paths are previously examined development windows
with costs imposed on a forecasting task. A prospective, operator-costed deployment is the next test. At budgets of $5\%$ or less no selective monitor reaches useful detection
under a strong intervention, so those regimes need more labels or a different signal.
