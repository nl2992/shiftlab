# Paper 3 candidate: budgeted active adaptation

This directory currently records prediction and adaptation-cost baselines. The candidate research
question in the [research charter](../../docs/research_charter.md) is broader: how to allocate finite
label, compute, and intervention budgets across observation, calibration, updating, retraining, and
fallback. Current periodic and triggered retraining comparisons are baseline evidence only; they do
not establish a novel controller or general regret guarantee.
