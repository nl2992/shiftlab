# Hsu–Shekhar: explicit sampling-contract exclusion

2026-10-30. [Efficient Sequential Evaluation of Large Language Models, arXiv2607.17409v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.17409), Sections1–3 and4.1–4.2 inspected in primary full text. This is a contract audit, not a native experiment reproduction or proof that every possible adaptation is inapplicable.

Their target is the mean of a fixed question-probability vector. At each evaluation step the learner selects an index from a predictable distribution q over all N question identities. The observation law is q(i)Bernoulli(p_i). Their RIPr projection in Eq4 and conditional e-value Proposition4.1 depend on this chosen categorical distribution; betting Eq8-style estimator uses a population prediction average plus inverse categorical propensity correction. Sections2–3 explicitly distinguish this from the stream setting where one decides whether to label the current covariate.

ShiftLab instead receives the current event in chronological order, chooses a Bernoulli query, waits for release and incurs deployment loss before future events can be inspected. It cannot choose a future event identity from a known population. These are different statistical experiments and different costs, not merely different hyperparameters.

Elementary obstruction to transplanting the algorithm unchanged: take two events with different future outcomes and identical observed history at t=1. A bank algorithm can assign positive query probability to identity2 at its first query; a chronological algorithm cannot observe event2's covariates/outcome before its arrival. Such a bank query is not measurable in ShiftLab's allowed information. Substituting a Bernoulli query probability into an N-category weight is also algebraically wrong: for two identities with risks0 and1 and categorical probabilities.9/.1, inverse categorical weighting estimates the bank mean.5; always observing identity1 with probability.9 and no access to identity2 cannot estimate that population mean using the same formula. The missing identity is not a delayed label from an issued request.

**Disposition:** exclude unchanged bank-selection RIPr/growth-mixture algorithms from the same-information chronological switching table. Record EXCLUDED_UNCHANGED_INFORMATION_CONTRACT rather than an unexecuted loss or a completed reproduction. A native fixed-bank comparison could be run as a separate task, but would not close a chronological utility claim. A finite-context reduction would require known population/context weights, a permissible sampling mechanism and a proof of the transformed target. We have not established those conditions for the current real streams.

The paper remains an important novelty constraint: growth-oriented querying, prediction mismatch/spikiness diagnostics and mixtures with uniform exploration are already explicit contributions. Merely mixing such rules to repair underspending or spiky allocation is not a defensible new mechanism. This does not prove the full delayed cost-switching problem reduces to that paper; a substantive residual must still be derived.
