# Integrated efficacy source ordering and freeze chronology (partial W03/W08)

The six source/protocol files in the run manifest match both their `execution_sources` snapshots and the current audit worktree byte for byte. This anchors the following source inspection to the archived experiment, rather than to a later implementation. It is a static data-flow inspection, not an independent simulator replay or formal filtration proof.

`_fit_candidate_family` fits the logistic predictor on `x[:400], y[:400]` and ranks candidate policies using validation outcomes `y[400:800]`. Future features are passed through frozen fitted models, but the candidate training/ranking path does not use future labels. In the integrated runner, the Q1 monitor reads queried outcomes only in events 800–1599 and stops at its first crossing or label cap. The scout uses queried outcomes in its early 1600–1999 slice to choose candidates; the fresh certification computation uses queried outcomes beginning at event 2000 and its crossing is evaluated before deployment at 2400. The fixed-candidate ablation fixes candidate 0 from validation and uses queried labels only in the Q2 certification slice. The controller's estimated gain uses queried certification outcomes. The rollback monitor starts at 2400 and reads queried deployment outcomes until its crossing or cap; its alarm-event action remains in place, with any rollback applied afterward. Synthetic oracle probabilities and unqueried outcomes enter evaluator summaries such as `h_truth`, expected utility, and realized future gain, but no reviewed branch making a promotion, controller, or rollback decision reads those evaluator summaries.

There are two important qualifications. First, `_build_event_logs` serializes the trace after `run_seed` computes the entire path. Its `prediction_decision_index` and `label_available_index` fields describe intended timing but are not an execution-time access control. Our event audit confirms internal trace consistency, not that a future label could never affect a decision through an unreviewed path or dependency. Second, the Q1 and rollback routines precompute full outcome-derived arrays; the reviewed loops index them only at queried current events. A targeted CUDA probe on `boundary_local_harm`/seed `2026104000` reproduces the archived promotion, label count, trigger, and expected utility. Flipping 1,456 unqueried post-fit labels leaves all 3,200 learner trace rows unchanged. Separately flipping all 800 deployment labels (152 queried) leaves every learner trace field before deployment unchanged. This is one stream and two perturbations, not a complete filtration proof; additional methods, profiles, seeds, and stepwise execution remain untested.
