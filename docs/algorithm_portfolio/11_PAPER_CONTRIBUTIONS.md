# Paper contributions (what the evidence supports)

A methods-and-analysis paper on budgeted, delayed model replacement:

1. **C1: Failure Atlas M1–M5** with exact contracts (02). M5, evidence-memory transfer failure, is
   new and is reproduced on sealed real data: arm A (λ=.95) beats arm B on 16/16.
2. **Method: confidence-bound reversible payback switching** under hard, delayed,
   disagreement-only label budgets (EQSDp ≡ Wald LCB). It is a NOVEL-INTEGRATION, presented as a
   practical, simple rule, not a new primitive.
3. **Synthetic evidence.**
   - Frozen C3: −8.8 / −9.7 vs Gong–Zhang-style / Qiu-style adapters.
   - Frontier analysis: 12 two-model mechanisms, BCR at K=3–5, Gap-Mix+ and PAVE+ all fail to
     beat simple, well-tuned rules.
   - Information-limit analysis: oracle change times are worth 11.7 units, doubling labels is
     worth 5.3, unlabeled signals ≤0.7.
4. **Real data (C4).**
   - 16 sealed datasets from 16 organisations; 9 meet the per-dataset criterion.
   - 16/16 favour the method; binomial count bound 1.6 × 10⁻⁸.
   - The effect is small (−0.75% of cost) and comes from halved switching.
   - Never-switching wins on 3 datasets.
5. **Negative-results record:** SAES, BCR, Gap-Mix+, PAVE+ and the label-only screens, each with
   a registered verdict.

Not claimable:
- a new algorithmic primitive;
- superiority over native Gong–Zhang, Qiu or UPF;
- false-promotion control;
- a 1%-plus real-data gain as a general expectation.
