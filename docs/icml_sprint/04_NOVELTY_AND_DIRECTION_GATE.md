**Update after reading the repository's own ledger and source table.** Direction B's neighbourhood is far more occupied than my search showed: hard-cap switching costs (Cheng), joint acquisition and detection with switching cost (Lau–Tay), sampling-right-constrained detection (Geng et al.), delayed-response sequential experimentation with adoption cost and a sample cap (Chick et al.), active budgeted testing (Li et al.). The kill tests on the repository's own candidate delta are still open, but the prior probability that a *general* joint acquire-and-switch rule survives them is low, and three earlier attempts (futility stopping, standard planning, recurrence forecasting) already reduced to known results. Direction A and C stay killed. The only option not killed on prior-art grounds in the files I read is a narrow, explicitly empirical one: Brier-guided cost-null betting (H2c) as an M6-style engine improvement, which the ledger classifies as non-claimable if it is a betting-engine variant. Net: **no direction currently clears the novelty gate.**
