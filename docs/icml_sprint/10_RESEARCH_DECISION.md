# 10. Research decision
**Assessment: `NO_DEFENSIBLE_METHOD_YET`.** Infrastructure and a replayed within-class empirical result exist; novelty and superiority over any published method are unproven; the lineage's main interpretive claim (decision-aware, low benign false alarms) was found to rest on an estimand mismatch.
**A. Established.** PROVED: validity of cost-null and restart e-processes under their stated assumptions; the ARL/finite-horizon distinction (SR at 1/α alarms on 100% of null streams over 2000 steps); H2c's frozen G5 gain over cost-only discounted Kelly/ONS replays (+0.086 / +0.094, 13/14 and 14/14 cells). EMPIRICAL, development only: Brier guidance transfers across cost-evidence parents; calibration size dominates power. FALSIFIED/withdrawn: HG's marginal-null validity with a context-dependent cap; the "benign false alarm" reading of the G5 results.
**B. Is H2c worth continued investment?** Not as a headline method. It reproduces within its class, its true size on null cells is ≈ .01 (conservative), but (i) it tests a zero-tolerance cost null, not the 0.10 economic harm, (ii) HG is more powerful at small real cost increases, (iii) its core may be covered by prediction-powered betting papers (unresolved), (iv) realistic nominal-risk calibration removes its power below ≈ 5,000 calibration events, (v) it has never run on real data.
**C–G.** Best new direction: B (question only). Strongest theorem: the finite-horizon restart/mixture supermartingale (known construction; no new result). Strongest experiment: G5 D1 replay (synthetic, within class). Finance application: sequential forecaster replacement under switching cost needs point-in-time data with verified label release times, which this environment cannot reach (hosts return 403). **ICML readiness: `NO_DEFENSIBLE_METHOD_YET`.**
**H. Next, in order of value.** (1) Re-run the cost-null comparison with a tolerance-aligned null (ε = 0.10, harm threshold matched) against HG/Brier ONS at matched economic tolerance — this decides whether H2c has any decision-aware advantage at all. (2) Direction-B kill test on the exact solvers (joint vs separately tuned policies, equal information). (3) Open the primary papers (needs host access) for PPRM, "Betting on Predictions" and the active-RCPS paper to settle H2c's novelty. Everything else (real data, G7/G8, Q2/Q3) waits on these.
