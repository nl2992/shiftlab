This adapts PPRM's running-risk bound to paired costs and omits its separate source-risk upper confidence bound. The adaptive-η block monitor is integrated into the hard-budget runner. The matched independent 200-stream comparison and the separate 2,000-stream registered-null calibration are complete and documented in [`pprm_paired_decision_benchmark.md`](pprm_paired_decision_benchmark.md), including the residual-variance harm reclassification.
